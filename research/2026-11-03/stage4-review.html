Model specification

Ten common normalized annual periods ending 3 November 2027 through 3 November 2036, five detailed plus five transition years. Revenue baselines use the midpoint of current FY guidance (EXLS 2,435; DOCU 3,503) as an annual run-rate anchor, not an actual trailing-twelve-month observation. Fiscal years do not end on 3 November; no fabricated stub actuals are used. This timing approximation and the stale July DOCU balance are limitations, with materially less impact than growth/margins/SBC assumptions.

For each year:

Revenue[t] = Revenue[t-1] × (1 + growth[t]). EBIT[t] = Revenue[t] × operating margin[t]. NOPAT[t] = EBIT[t] × (1 - tax rate). FCFF[t] = NOPAT[t] + D&A[t] - economic capex[t] - change in operating working capital[t]. Enterprise value = present value of years 1–10 FCFF plus discounted terminal value. Terminal FCFF = NOPAT[10] × (1 + g) × (1 - g / terminal ROIC). Terminal EV = terminal FCFF / (WACC - g). Equity value = EV + excess cash - debt - other financial claims.

Tax rate is an analyst-normalized 24% for both, not company guidance. Discount rates are explicit nominal-USD required-return assumptions, not claimed empirically estimated CAPM WACCs. EXLS’s 9.5% central rate reflects labor/FX, acquisition and execution risks with modest net leverage. DOCU’s 10% reflects competitive/AI execution uncertainty despite no funded debt. The ±1-point sensitivities are essential, not precision intervals. Terminal growth stays below each discount rate; ROIC funds continuing investment rather than assuming free terminal growth.
