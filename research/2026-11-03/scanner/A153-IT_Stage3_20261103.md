## Model construction
Ten annual forward periods, 5 explicit + 5 transition, with end-year discounting. Annual 2026 guide is the starting run-rate; year 1 is a forward year near 2027. This is not a two-month stub model. No future share-count reductions or purchased growth are assumed.

FCFF = EBIT × (1 − tax) + D&A − reinvestment expenditure − change in operating working capital. Adjusted EBITDA excludes SBC, so SBC is subtracted exactly once to reach EBIT. Adjusted EPS already retains SBC and is not used as an unadjusted cash-flow starting point.

Central assumptions: revenue $6,405m; first-five-year growth 3%, then 2.9/2.8/2.7/2.6/2.5%; adjusted EBITDA margin starts at 1,615/6,405 = 25.2147% and converges to 25.2% in year 5; SBC 177/6,405 = 2.7635%; D&A 181/6,405 = 2.8260%; tax 24%; initial capex/software 1.7% of sales, above the $95m FY2026 guide; working capital 2% of incremental sales. Organic content and research investment remains in payroll/operating expense. The first-five-year negative net capital reinvestment is consistent with acquired-amortization addbacks exceeding physical/software capex, but is not continued indefinitely.

Years 6–10 blend the explicit reinvestment formula toward NOPAT × annual growth / 20% incremental ROIC, with 20/40/60/80/100% transition weights. Terminal FCFF = year-11 NOPAT × (1 − 2.5%/20%). The 20% ROIC is an assumption, not measured accounting ROIC. Terminal value is 48.94% of operating EV. Central year-1 FCFF is $1,054.268m and year-10 FCFF $1,106.288m.

Cost of equity remains 10.5%, assumed refinancing debt cost 5.5%, tax shield 24%. At $204.22 × 64m equity and $3,000m debt, WACC is 9.3202%. These are research-required returns, not an observed CAPM estimate. The effective historical debt rate is about 4.34%, and all debt is fixed rate. Principal maturities are $800m in 2028, $600m in 2029, $800m in 2030, $350m in 2031 and $450m in 2035. An undrawn $1bn revolver supports liquidity. [2]–[3]

Equity bridge: operating EV $13,936.116m + September cash $1,633.137m − $500m operating reserve − $3,000m principal = $12,069.253m / 64m diluted shares = $188.58. The 64m share count is the September supplement's diluted figure. Do not combine the lower October 30 basic count with September cash without subtracting later buybacks. Operating leases remain in costs and are not deducted again as debt. 66% of cash is outside the US; no-excess-cash credit reduces central value to $170.88. Pension/other non-operating liabilities are small relative to the decision threshold but no unrestricted-cash certainty is asserted.
