## Decision

**WAIT_PRICE. Stage 3 research complete; no Stage 3.5 entry, order, stop or position size approved.** EQT merits valuation because of its integrated cost structure, positive cash generation and inventory. The Range acquisition is analyzed, not used as a reason to abandon research. At the scanner price, neither the standalone central case nor the conditional merged central case establishes a margin of safety.

Standalone central value is **$33.97 per share**, with a central-deck WACC range of approximately **$28–41**. The merger-conditional value is **$32.89**, with a comparable range of approximately **$28–40**. These ranges vary discount rates from 11% to 9%; they are not statistical confidence intervals. The broad bull/bear cases below are separate economic regimes. No arbitrary percentage haircut or invented merger-closing probability is applied.

Current price can be justified by a plausible stronger gas market. The same-model reverse requires 2027 Henry Hub around **$3.80 standalone or $3.88 merged**, versus the central $3.16 assumption, sustained higher thereafter. Therefore the conclusion is insufficient demonstrated safety margin, not certainty that the market is wrong. Confidence is **medium in WAIT_PRICE and low-to-medium in a point valuation**.

## Business, cycle and why a low P/E can mislead

EQT produces predominantly Appalachian gas and owns gathering, transmission and storage assets. Consolidation removes a portion of third-party gathering costs, but does not eliminate depletion, maintenance spending, debt or minority ownership. Range adds substantial liquids-rich Marcellus acreage and production. Management describes a combined platform above 9 Bcfe/day, roughly 2.8 million net acres and more than 30 years of inventory. Inventory is not the same as proved reserves or free perpetual production. [Merger presentation, October 26](https://s205.q4cdn.com/630272887/files/doc_downloads/2026/EQT-and-Range-Combine-to-Create-America-s-Natural-Gas-Champion.pdf).

EQT's 2025 proved reserves were about **28 Tcfe**; Range reported **18.142 Tcfe**, including **5.341 Tcfe undeveloped**. Converting inventory requires drilling capital, permits, infrastructure and economic commodity prices. This analysis retains replacement spending, separately tests finite continuation, and does not capitalize the reserve book value on top of cash flow. [EQT FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/33213/000003321326000018/eqt-20251231.htm); [Range FY2025 10-K](https://ir.rangeresources.com/static-files/e5f5dc37-2ac2-48cb-ba42-8066a261f002).

EQT's Q3 volume fell **8.1% to 582.802 Bcfe**, including 21 Bcfe of strategic curtailments. Adjusted EBITDA attributable to EQT fell to **$1.021bn** from $1.200bn; attributable FCF fell to **$283m** from $484m. The $1.143bn consolidated adjusted EBITDA is not all attributable to common shareholders. Q3 unit operating cost was $1.09/Mcfe, versus $1.00 a year earlier. The company remains profitable, but that quarter is not evidence of smooth organic earnings growth. [EQT Q3 2026 earnings release, October 27](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/33213/000003321326000054/eqt-ex991earningsreleasexl.htm).

Annual Henry Hub spot averages were **$6.45/$2.53/$2.19/$3.52 per MMBtu in 2022/23/24/25**. That peak-to-trough cycle is much wider than a one-quarter EPS comparison suggests. The central model starts from the current EIA 2027 forecast and transitions to a steady real price; neither the 2022 peak nor the 2024 trough is mechanically capitalized forever. [EIA historical annual Henry Hub spot prices](https://www.eia.gov/dnav/ng/NG_PRI_FUT_S1_A.htm).

The merger model adds Range's assets/cash flows, debt and ownership claims **and** the new shares. It does not subtract stock consideration as a second cash purchase price. Pre-close retained operating cash is treated as part of assets delivered; a $33m Range pre-close distribution reserve is deducted. Unknown final purchase accounting and intercompany revenue eliminations are not fabricated. Combining net cash margins avoids inventing extra profit from payments between the two companies.

## Catalysts, disconfirming evidence and what would change the decision

1. **Merger documentation and approvals:** S-4/joint proxy, final share/award count, financial pro formas, shareholder meetings and actual HSR status. First-quarter 2027 is management's target, not a promised date. A delay affects synergy timing and integration cash; regulatory divestitures would require rebuilding the asset/cash-flow perimeter.
2. **Commodity and basis evidence:** EIA's next outlook is due November 10. Follow storage, winter realizations, regional constraints and the company's curtailment decisions. Higher prices must survive the maintenance/cash-tax bridge, not simply lift mark-to-market EPS.
3. **2027 capital plan and reserves:** verify replacement spending, field productivity and the Range standalone/combined maintenance requirement. Higher sustained capital than modeled directly lowers value, as the joint reverse shows.
4. **PipeBox waterfall:** reconcile distributions, incremental capital and remaining Base Return. A low reported debt ratio does not make the minority cash claim disappear.
5. **POWER and LNG:** the November 3 communication says the POWER open season has just closed; demand evaluation is not a contracted, permitted or commissioned pipeline. Watch binding customers, FID, construction, liquefaction schedules and net sales margins. No additional 2 MTPA is credited in base. [SEC merger communication, November 3](https://ir.rangeresources.com/static-files/dff3242e-1d23-4e03-b2b2-686e6d5bfb96).
6. **Thesis failure:** sustained wider basis, rising sustaining cost, weaker gas/NGL demand, LNG toll/shipping losses, material merger remedies, poor integration or more dilution without cash growth. Favorable evidence would be contracted attractive netbacks, demonstrated lower replacement cost, a stronger funded cash trajectory, or a lower share price with the technical trend still acceptable.
