# AGCO historical financial rebuild — 2026-11-04

Run `python rebuild_history.py` (Python standard library only, offline). Requires only numeric_inputs.json. Outputs history.json, history.csv, ledger.csv and qa.txt. Public package allowlist: README.md, numeric_inputs.json, rebuild_history.py, history.json, history.csv, ledger.csv, qa.txt, comparability.md and limited_ebit_bridge.json. Exclude sources/ (private full documents) and prepare_inputs.py (private extraction helper requiring full companyfacts).

USD millions unless EPS (USD/share), diluted shares (millions) or ratio (%). FY2019–FY2025, Q4 2024 through Q3 2026, TTM September 2025 and 2026. Financial income and balance figures use latest SEC facts available on the as-of date; filings with a later date are excluded. Latest comparative disclosures can reclassify prior figures. URLs and original US-GAAP tags appear in ledger.csv; SEC accession directory links identify the source filing. Numeric-input records retain accession, form and filing date.

NetIncomeLoss was verified as income attributable to AGCO, not consolidated income including NCI. Q3 2026 attributable NI is 51.7, versus total NI 50.0. EBIT means reported operating income, not segment operating profit or pretax earnings. Affiliate income is below EBIT. Debt is balance-sheet carrying amount current plus noncurrent borrowings, excluding operating leases and redeemable NCI; capital/equity valuation requires considering those separately.

Quarterly cash-flow metrics derive from cumulative YTD less preceding YTD (Q4 = FY less 9M). Direct three-month income-statement facts are used wherever available. Q4 2024 and Q4 2025 diluted EPS come directly from the FY2025 earnings release. EPS is NEVER calculated by subtracting annual and nine-month EPS. TTM EPS is explicitly a sum of four direct reported quarterly EPS (not a directly reported TTM EPS and not NI/latest shares). Diluted weighted shares are null where no direct period disclosure exists.

FCF proxy = CFO minus reported purchases of PPE; after-SBC proxy subtracts cash-flow stock-compensation adjustment. Separately capitalized software cash spending is not identified in reviewed cash-flow statements. It is null, not zero, and no claim of a fully all-in software-inclusive FCF is made. D&A sums depreciation and acquired-intangible amortization. All GAAP amounts remain unadjusted for portfolio change, impairments, asset-sale gains, taxes or tariffs. See comparability.md.

QA reconciles debt/cash formulas, balance sheet including redeemable NCI where available (0.2m rounding tolerance), and each TTM key flow against the four quarters. Historical noncontrolling equity can account for small difference between parent equity and consolidated assets/liabilities. All latest three FY and latest four quarters have the requested core metrics populated. Sources are SEC XBRL companyfacts, annual/quarterly SEC statements and Q4 earnings release; financial facts are not investment forecasts.
