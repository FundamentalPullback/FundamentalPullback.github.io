# Comparability, not a valuation

1. Cycle: reported sales increased from $9,041.4m in 2019 to $14,412.4m in 2023, before falling to $11,661.9m in 2024 and $10,082.0m in 2025. TTM September 2026 sales are $10,135.3m. This is not a same-business organic series: PTx Trimble entered consolidation on April 1, 2024 (85% owned) and most Grain & Protein was sold November 1, 2024. Segment geography also changed in 2026 with Mexico reassigned to Latin America, with comparative segment data recast.

2. G&P was NOT discontinued operations. The 2025 10-K note 3 says the sale did not constitute a strategic shift with a major effect. Accordingly prior consolidated sales and results retain that business. 2024 sales include $816.5m of disposed G&P revenue. The business-sale loss was $507.3m in 2024, with $12.3m additional loss finalized in May 2025; full-year 2025 net loss on business sales was $10.8m after an unrelated immaterial divestiture gain. The sale had a $700m headline price and $630.7m net closing proceeds before final adjustments. Do not confuse sale proceeds with recurring CFO or treat all lower sales as demand erosion.

3. Reported EBIT improvement is heavily distorted by 2024 charges. FY2024 EBIT −$122.1m includes $369.5m impairment and $507.3m business-sale loss. FY2025 EBIT $595.7m includes $10.0m impairment and $10.8m business-sale loss. Removing ONLY these two lines gives $754.7m and $616.5m respectively, not an operating earnings improvement. This intentionally retains restructuring, SBC, acquired-intangible amortization, transaction expenses and all other effects; it is not adjusted or normalized EBIT.

4. The same limited bridge produces TTM Sep2025 EBIT $489.5m versus reported $96.2m, and TTM Sep2026 $571.5m versus reported $568.2m. Prior TTM charges are $371.5m impairment plus $21.8m sale loss; current TTM has $4.8m impairment and a $1.5m sale gain. The comparable limited-bridge growth is approximately 16.8%, rather than the roughly 491% reported growth. This does not prove cyclical normalization or eliminate recurring restructuring. Exact numeric inputs, dates, source URLs and bridge formulas are in numeric_inputs.json/rebuild_history.py/limited_ebit_bridge.json.

5. FY2025 NI/EPS $726.5m/$9.75 are not a sustainable run-rate. The TAFE sale on September 30, 2025 generated a $251.9m pretax nonoperating gain ($260m proceeds). Q3 2025 non-GAAP reconciliation removes $219.2m after-tax gain, or $2.93/share. Legal-entity tax reorganization also materially benefited 2025. These gains are below EBIT and must not be added to the EBIT bridge. Q3 2026 EPS $0.75 vs $4.09 is therefore not a 82% fall in recurring earnings; adjusted EPS $1.05 vs $1.35 is more informative but still includes management exclusions and tariff-refund effects. No automatic substitution of adjusted EPS is made in the ledger.

6. FCF is seasonal and impacted by working capital. FY2025 CFO $988.1m/PPE $247.9m implies PPE-based FCF $740.2m; TTM Sep2026 CFO $545.2m/PPE $250.8m implies $294.4m. Q4 2025 alone contributes $675.0m FCF and 9M2026 contributes −$380.6m. Current Q3 FCF is −$33.8m. Strong fourth-quarter collections cannot be annualized blindly, and TAFE investment-sale proceeds are investing cash flows, not CFO. Separate software cash capex is not disclosed in reviewed statements, so all FCF measures remain labelled PPE proxies.

7. Rabobank finance affiliates complicate industrial interpretations: AGCO historically owns 49% in finance equity JVs, with Rabobank controlling and funding them. Affiliate earnings sit below operating income. Wholesale-receivable sales, financing subsidies, guarantees and subsequent changes in JV ownership deserve separate scrutiny. Do not consolidate all JV loans into AGCO industrial debt or treat monetizations as recurring revenue. On April 30, 2026, AGCO sold its 49% U.S. and Canadian finance JV equity interests to Rabobank subsidiaries for $188.4m. About $20m of consideration related to future portfolio run-off earnings was recognized in other income during 9M2026. Thus current NI is also affected by monetization, not only the prior-year TAFE gain.

8. Q3 2026 North American operating income benefited by approximately $13m in IEEPA tariff refunds according to the release. Those refunds are not stripped from management adjusted EPS; recovery and retention are uncertain. Do not assume no tariff benefit simply because the GAAP/non-GAAP bridge lacks an exclusion.
