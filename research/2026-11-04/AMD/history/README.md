Acquired intangible amortization is separately 2,811/2,393/2,254 (cashflow presentation) and 2,186 current TTM. Total D&A is the sum, never add acquired amortization twice. FY2024 income statement acquired amortization is 2,394 versus cashflow 2,393; corresponding Q4FY2024 584 versus 583. Preserve this $1m presentation difference rather than force a reconciliation. FY2025 Note 3 separately reports PPE depreciation 441/454/521 for FY2023/24/25; CFS ex-acquisition D&A is not synonymous with PPE depreciation.

There is no separately disclosed capitalized-software cash addition line in the reviewed statements/notes. Use disclosed PPE purchases as the observable capex line; do not claim software spend is zero or invent an increment. R&D tax capitalization is not book capitalized-software cash capex. Acquisitions and investment purchases are not PPE capex and are maintained separately.
