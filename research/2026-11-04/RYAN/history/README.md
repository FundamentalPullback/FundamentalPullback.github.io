# RYAN financial history, cutoff 2026-11-04

Rebuild offline: python history/build_history.py; python history/build_kpis.py; python history/qa_history.py from any cwd. Public offline rebuild requires only Python standard library and the included factual_inputs.json/kpi_inputs.json. Source extraction scripts requiring lxml and original SEC HTML are private research material in sources_private/. Private normalize.py extracts context dates, dimensional axes, tagged amounts, units and original fact IDs. source_lineage.json records exact chosen filing/fact for every raw input. Latest available same-period filing wins, then earliest fact in document (primary financial statements before rounded note excerpts). Facts after cutoff excluded. Source manifests: sources_private/submissions.json; accession-based IR or SEC links.

normalized_history.csv contains FY2023/24/25, Q4'25/Q1–3'26 and TTM September2025/2026. USD millions. Blank means unavailable, not zero. Quarter cash-flow values are YTD differences; Q4 is FY less nine months. TTM is previous FY plus current9M minus previous9M. Fiscal years end December31. No projected Q4'26 is included. Non-GAAP quarterly sums can differ from reported YTD/FY by USD0.001M rounding; QA allows0.002M for those identities. reported_kpis.json contains company-defined non-GAAP measures and organic growth, with exact source table index; not comparable with GAAP EBIT or analyst-normalized EBITA.

Important normalization limits:
- ebita_ex_remeasurement_retains_sbc_restructuring = GAAP operating income + total amortization + contingent-consideration remeasurement. It retains restructuring, SBC, transaction/retention and incentive costs. TOTAL amortization includes internally developed software, not exclusively acquired intangible amortization. Do not label it acquired-only EBITA. If adding it back in cash flow, subtract adequate internal software/capital expenditure replacement investment.
- Q3'26 contingent remeasurement was a gain10.658; adding signed expense removes this gain. TTM remeasurement expense50.142 versus priorTTM gain26.505 distorts raw EBIT growth. A normalized EBITA margin must not erase contingent purchase-price obligations in the balance-sheet bridge.
- Q3 PSU expense was -9.221 and9M -5.778; Q3 reversal not sustainable compensation run rate. No invented exact future SBC rate. Cash-flow SBC51.9689M includes IPO awards; company adjusted EBITDAC adds back equity comp45.825 plus IPO-related6.143 separately. Do not add these twice.
- Empower expense61.9829M includes software impairment11.704; total restructuring adjustments63.149 in company reconciliation include other restructuring. Rollforward charges83.624 include21.642 capitalized spending. Capex already captures capitalized outlays. Do not add back impairment twice, promise full80 cost savings for free, or charge total remaining program again when the run-rate already retains its expenses.
- cfo_less_capex is consolidated levered cash flow, NOT free cash flow for Class A. CFO includes actual mixed Up-C taxes, working capital, contingent operating settlements and noncash SBC additions. Financing includes distributions to external LLC holders and fiduciary-liability movements. No annualization of Q1 seasonal cash outflow.
- The SBC-subtracted CFO proxy is a sensitivity, not audited owner earnings; it does not solve tax normalization, NCI, TRA, acquisition reinvestment, or dilution.
- Cash-flow contingent operating payments may only be disclosed to one decimal million and are missing in some periods. Do not silently force a missing prior period to zero.
- Financial statements and share dates differ; see capital_research/capital_findings.md.
