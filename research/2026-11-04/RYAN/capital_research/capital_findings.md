## Separate Holdings-only bridge

Note 12 identifies Holdings-only cash45.5; other current assets6.4; deferred tax assets251.2; AP/accruals4.7; other noncurrent liabilities4.0; TRA485.1. Consolidated free corporate cash168.811 therefore includes opco cash123.311. Other assets/liabilities and performance substantially represent LLC.

A rigorous bridge values 100% LLC economics, deducts LLC obligations, allocates economic value to Holdings, then adds/deducts Holdings-only items. Do not divide consolidated equity value by A shares alone. Do not also deduct book NCI455.033 if the model already allocates economic ownership or uses all economic-equivalent units. Do not allocate the entire TRA across A+B: it is a Holdings-only claim.

Parent DTA251.2 is an accounting tax asset, not cash. Model tax savings and TRA together, or explicitly label a conservative treatment. Adding DTA face value and also reducing modeled cash taxes counts its benefits twice.

## TRA

Company pays 85% of qualifying realized/deemed tax savings, retains15%. Estimated savings570.7 imply retained85.6 undiscounted; liability reported undiscounted. ## Deferred employee obligations: do not silently omit

At 30 September employee deferred compensation liability was9.2 current+63.6 noncurrent=72.8. FY2025 Employee Benefits note says deferred amounts remain company assets subject to creditor claims and are NOT invested in any funds; liability tracks hypothetical benchmark investment returns. No dedicated matched investment asset may be assumed. Withdrawals generally occur3–10years after deferral, timing uncertain. A DCF based on accrual EBIT without a settlement adjustment should assess deduction of the opening obligation as debt-like; don't then charge its settlement again in projected working capital. New future compensation expense remains operating expense.

Separate acquisition-related long-term incentive agreements: accrued8.982 current+21.476 noncurrent=30.458; projected FUTURE expense26.697; total future cash outflows57.155. Payment schedule: remaining2026 10.124;2027 8.747;2028 28.812;2029 1.801;thereafter7.671. If retaining future incentive expense in forecast EBIT, avoid also deducting the full57.155 as an incremental debt. Distinguish opening accrued30.458 from future expense26.697. Both balances are within accrued compensation, so an all-working-capital bridge must not deduct them a second time.
