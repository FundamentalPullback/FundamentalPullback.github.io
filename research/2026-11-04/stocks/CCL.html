Research cutoff: November 4, 2026, approximately 04:15 UTC. Reference prices are the November 3 scanner inputs supplied for this task, not live executable quotes: RCL $313.46; CCL $29.16. Decision

RCL: Stage 3 complete; do not select at the reference price under the stated return and reinvestment assumptions. The business has better margins and substantial growth opportunities, but the price requires much more long-lived profitable growth than the base case. The Sandals acquisition and ship payments must be financed alongside existing claims. This is a valuation decision, not an incomplete-research label.

CCL: Stage 3 complete; retain as the nearer valuation watch candidate, but do not select at the reference price under the 10% base WACC. It has a lower valuation and stronger near-term cash conversion, but that cash conversion benefits from a light delivery year. A 9% WACC nearly supports the scanner price; 10–11% does not. An investment case therefore needs explicit agreement on the required return and durable returns on new spending, rather than relying on the headline P/E.

Neither conclusion implies a short recommendation, a trade, or a proven technical setup. Stage 3.5 requires separate, current market/sector conditions, actual trend and support, and an executable entry/stop/target. There is no universal 15% discount rule here.

Consistent treatment

  • Deposits are operating liabilities, not excess cash or ordinary debt. They are captured through working capital and future voyage costs. Subtracting all deposits as debt after also modeling those obligations would double count. The model gives no recurring cash-flow bonus from rising deposit balances.
  • Analyst operating-cash reserves are 0.50 RCL and 0.75 CCL. Only the remaining 0.351/0.470 is credited as excess cash. These are assumptions, not disclosed minimum-cash requirements.
  • Core enterprise-to-equity claims are therefore 22.721 RCL = 22.872 − 0.351 + 0.200 minority interest; 24.108 CCL = 24.559 − 0.470 + 0.019 minority interest. Minority interests use carrying values as approximations.
  • Operating rent stays in EBIT/FCFF, so operating lease liabilities are not deducted again. Finance leases already included in debt are not added twice. A lease-capitalized alternative would require adjusting both cash flow and claims.
  • Ship capex is charged in forecast FCFF, not subtracted again as a lump-sum debt claim. Future borrowing is financing, not a source of FCFF. Likewise, don’t both subtract current debt and deduct its principal amortization inside FCFF.
  • An unmodeled 1.0 additional equity-funded liability reduces value by approximately $3.73 RCL / $0.73 CCL, before any operating effect. Extra 10% dilution reduces per-share value approximately 9.1%, all else equal.

6. Decision and confidence

The required historical coverage is now supplied. The data supports ongoing operating recovery, but not using the full reported net-income growth rate as perpetual operating growth. Historical-source confidence is high; the tax-adjusted normalization and capital-cycle forecast warrant moderate confidence, and the scenario range remains wide because of demand, pricing, fuel, tax, reinvestment and leverage. The original reasonable-price analysis remains conditional: a supported lower discount rate or stronger combination of operating assumptions can matter, and a partial entry does not require an arbitrary universal 15% discount.
