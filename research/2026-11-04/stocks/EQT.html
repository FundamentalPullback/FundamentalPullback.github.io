Business, cycle and why a low P/E can mislead

EQT produces predominantly Appalachian gas and owns gathering, transmission and storage assets. Consolidation removes a portion of third-party gathering costs, but does not eliminate depletion, maintenance spending, debt or minority ownership. Range adds substantial liquids-rich Marcellus acreage and production. Management describes a combined platform above 9 Bcfe/day, roughly 2.8 million net acres and more than 30 years of inventory. Inventory is not the same as proved reserves or free perpetual production. Merger presentation, October 26.

EQT’s 2025 proved reserves were about 28 Tcfe; Range reported 18.142 Tcfe, including 5.341 Tcfe undeveloped. Converting inventory requires drilling capital, permits, infrastructure and economic commodity prices. This analysis retains replacement spending, separately tests finite continuation, and does not capitalize the reserve book value on top of cash flow. EQT FY2025 10-K; Range FY2025 10-K.

EQT’s Q3 volume fell 8.1% to 582.802 Bcfe, including 21 Bcfe of strategic curtailments. Adjusted EBITDA attributable to EQT fell to $1.021bn from $1.200bn; attributable FCF fell to $283m from $484m. The $1.143bn consolidated adjusted EBITDA is not all attributable to common shareholders. Q3 unit operating cost was $1.09/Mcfe, versus $1.00 a year earlier. The company remains profitable, but that quarter is not evidence of smooth organic earnings growth. EQT Q3 2026 earnings release, October 27.

Catalysts, disconfirming evidence and what would change the decision

  1. Merger documentation and approvals: S-4/joint proxy, final share/award count, financial pro formas, shareholder meetings and actual HSR status. First-quarter 2027 is management’s target, not a promised date. A delay affects synergy timing and integration cash; regulatory divestitures would require rebuilding the asset/cash-flow perimeter.
  2. Commodity and basis evidence: EIA’s next outlook is due November 10. Follow storage, winter realizations, regional constraints and the company’s curtailment decisions. Higher prices must survive the maintenance/cash-tax bridge, not simply lift mark-to-market EPS.
  3. 2027 capital plan and reserves: verify replacement spending, field productivity and the Range standalone/combined maintenance requirement. Higher sustained capital than modeled directly lowers value, as the joint reverse shows.
  4. PipeBox waterfall: reconcile distributions, incremental capital and remaining Base Return. A low reported debt ratio does not make the minority cash claim disappear.
  5. POWER and LNG: the November 3 communication says the POWER open season has just closed; demand evaluation is not a contracted, permitted or commissioned pipeline. Watch binding customers, FID, construction, liquefaction schedules and net sales margins. No additional 2 MTPA is credited in base. SEC merger communication, November 3.
  6. Thesis failure: sustained wider basis, rising sustaining cost, weaker gas/NGL demand, LNG toll/shipping losses, material merger remedies, poor integration or more dilution without cash growth. Favorable evidence would be contracted attractive netbacks, demonstrated lower replacement cost, a stronger funded cash trajectory, or a lower share price with the technical trend still acceptable.

Completion, remaining uncertainty and execution boundary

This is a completed, reproducible research-stage WAIT, with a conditional merged model because the deal has not closed. Unobservable future commodity prices, final SEC transaction accounting, detailed synergy cost split, monthly hedge settlement paths, future cash taxes and field-level decline are bounded assumptions, not fabricated facts. The current share proxies and estimated ARO/Eureka claims are disclosed. A 9%–11% WACC range, capital/gas joint reverse, LNG scenarios, finite-life values and separate event branches expose the main sensitivities.

The scanner’s approximately 20.5% pullback does not prove a valid long-term technical entry. No fresh EMA/trend, stop, correlation or portfolio-size approval is made here. Research publication changes no holding and places no trade.
