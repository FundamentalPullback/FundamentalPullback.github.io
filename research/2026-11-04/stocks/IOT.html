What is attractive about the business

Samsara links vehicles, equipment, cameras, workflows and data for fleets and other physical operations. Safety, compliance, fuel efficiency and equipment utilization can create measurable customer value. Installed devices, enterprise integrations, operational history and multi-application adoption make switching more involved than replacing a simple dashboard. This supports a quality premium; it does not prove permanent monopoly pricing or costless AI growth.

Q2 revenue was$508.4m, up30%, ARR$2.125bn, up30%, and net new ARR$134.1m, up28%. Customers spending over$1m ARR contributed more than$500m ARR, growing over50% for a third consecutive quarter. Gross margin was77% GAAP and78% non-GAAP. FY2027 revenue guidance is$2.043bn–$2.047bn,26% growth, and non-GAAP operating margin21%; Q3 revenue guidance514–516m implies24% growth. The base model anchors on the annual revenue midpoint, not on annualizing one strong quarter. Q2 metrics and guidance

At FY2026 end,3,194 customers exceeded$100k ARR and supplied about61% of ARR; more than95% used at least two applications. “Core” now means$25k ARR rather than$10k, and customer aggregation changed in FY2026 to combine some affiliated entities. Old raw customer-count series must therefore be restated before inferring acceleration. Subscription agreements typically last3–5 years. RPO at August1 was$4.285bn, of which$1.839bn was expected in the next12 months; RPO, ARR and revenue are distinct measures. No one customer accounted for more than10% of reported revenue. A current numerical net-retention rate is not assumed where it is not furnished in the reviewed filings. Customer economics and definitions; Revenue and obligations notes

Competition includes private Motive and Geotab, OEM telematics, larger industrial/software platforms and specialist fleet suppliers. Camera/driver-monitoring data create privacy, biometric-law and cybersecurity exposure. Trucking, construction and logistics activity affect customer expansion and budgets. Land-and-expand economics must survive longer enterprise sales cycles and a less forgiving spending backdrop.

What would change the assessment

At December3 results, prioritize net-new ARR, large-account expansion and revenue guidance, then GAAP operating leverage after SBC, gross margin after cloud/device costs, capitalized device/commission balances, cash conversion excluding working-capital swings, and the fully diluted share count. An earlier durable move toward high-single-digit and then mid-teens economic margins would improve value much more than another adjusted-earnings beat created by addbacks.

To support current pricing without an extreme discount rate, evidence needs to move several assumptions: a longer period of roughly25%–30% growth, materially faster after-SBC profit conversion, or demonstrably lower recurring capital/customer-acquisition investment. Sustained ARR deceleration, hardware/cloud margin pressure, rising compensation dilution, lower customer renewal/expansion or litigation/privacy costs weaken the thesis. A near-term quarter alone cannot prove or falsify a ten-year platform outcome, but it can change the burden of proof.

Stage3 is complete; Stage3.5 has no entry approval. November3’s41.11 close was below October26’s44.65 and followed volatile October trading. That does not alone establish a valid long-term pullback/reversal. If fundamentals later qualify, separately verify broad market/software conditions, trend and relative strength, event timing, a concrete invalidation level and an actual risk budget. Trade3:1 means (credible target −entry)/(entry −technical stop), including execution/gap risk. Neither the$5.67 fundamental bear scenario nor the$44.02 long-horizon bull value is a technical stop/short-term target. Do not force a3:1 by borrowing those numbers.
