# IRDM financial history, as of November 4, 2026

## Use and reproduction
Run `python reproduce.py` from any directory. This uses only the Python standard library and adjacent factual JSON files. It requires neither network access nor SEC filings. Output is `normalized_history.csv`, `normalized_history.json`, and `derivation_audit.json`. Values are USD millions or millions of shares, except per-share figures. Null means unavailable or not separately disclosed, never zero.

The factual ledger records original numeric facts, SEC source URLs, XBRL concept, context, dimensions, dates, and precision. `manual_facts.json` supplies untagged debt disclosures. `disclosure_ledger.json` contains selected release KPIs and transaction context. `sources.json` identifies primary filings. Full filings, extracted source text and download/extraction scripts remain in the sibling `sources/` folder and are not needed or intended for the public artifact.

## Scope
Three full years (2023–25), four latest quarters (Q4 2025–Q3 2026), 9M 2026/2025 and TTM September 2026/2025. Revenue categories are revenue disaggregation, not independently valued operating segments. The revenue categories service + equipment + engineering reconcile to total revenue. Commercial voice, IoT, broadband and hosted/other are a subset of service revenue, not the whole service total. Government service and engineering are distinguishable in source filings but not separately normalized here.

## Derivations and limits
- Q4 = full year minus first nine months. Q2/Q3 cash-flow metrics = cumulative cash flows minus prior cumulative period. Directly reported quarterly income-statement figures take priority.
- TTM = preceding FY plus current nine months minus prior nine months. These are historical acquired-perimeter numbers, not a combined-company pro forma or annualized run rate.
- Q4 and TTM EPS are calculated, not reported GAAP EPS. EPS is not summed or subtracted. Duration-weighted share counts are inferred from cumulative averages using calendar days, then NI is divided by those counts. Dilution mechanics can vary between cumulative and quarterly periods, so these synthetic denominators are approximations and unsuitable for claiming an exact reported EPS. Reported quarterly and full-year EPS is retained.
- Cash capex is the gross cash acquisition of PP&E, before asset-sale proceeds. CFO less cash capex is a levered historical cash-flow measure, not unlevered DCF cash flow. Acquisition cash payments are separate investing flows, not recurring capex.
- No SBC is removed from EBIT or NI. SBC expense in the cash-flow statement is distinct from capitalized SBC; their sum reconciles to total equity compensation recognized within $0.001M source rounding. Q3 2026 cash-flow differencing yields $20.232M SBC, whereas the earnings-release reconciliation reports $20.231M. This $1,000 difference is retained transparently rather than overwritten. The prior TTM SBC check is $0.001M from source rounding.
- `transaction_cost_addback` is a narrow, separately identified scenario only. Q3 $21.294M and nine-month $36.318M come from the earnings release. The TTM $36.318M assumes the disclosed 2026 costs only; it is not an exhaustive independent recategorization of every prior-quarter cost. No tax-normalized NI is fabricated. Costs may recur until transaction completion, and adjusted EBIT is not cash flow.
- The Q3 annual-incentive shift adds $4.5M cash-compensation expense relative to the prior mix. It is an ongoing change in compensation economics, not automatically an exceptional addback. Keep separate from transaction costs and SBC.
- FY2023–25 PP&E depreciation is disclosed rounded to $0.1M. `da_less_identifiable_intangible_amortization` subtracts rounded identifiable-intangible amortization from reported total D&A. It includes equipment, software and satellite assets; it is NOT satellite-only physical depreciation. Quarterly satellite-only depreciation and software capitalization flows are not disclosed in the inspected filings and remain null. Gross software asset balances are stocks, not capitalization flow, and their change must not be used as a cash-capex proxy.
- Debt gross and debt net of issuance costs are distinct. Q1/Q2 2026 gross debt is available at $0.1M narrative precision; Q3 release gross debt is $2,159.458M and includes revolving credit and Aireon seller/acquired loans. Period-end cash $97.853M yields $2,061.605M net debt. Use the dedicated capital bridge for transaction valuation rather than confusing carrying debt with settlement amounts.

## Comparability and accounting flags
Aireon was equity-accounted until the remaining 60.5% was acquired July 2, 2026 for about $366.7M. Q3 and TTM September 2026 include only about one quarter of Aireon consolidation, while period-end debt and assets include the acquisition. Q3 Aireon revenue since acquisition is $34.492M. MD&A explicitly attributes $4.5M additional Q3 depreciation and $3.8M additional intangible amortization to Aireon. These are acquisition contributions, not a satellite-only depreciation breakdown. The earnings-release equity-method gain is $103.868M, dominated by remeasurement at control; nine-month equity-method gain is $101.626M after earlier losses. Do not extrapolate this gain, mistake it for operating profit, or deduct it from EBIT (it was below EBIT).

Satelles was acquired April 1, 2024, creating a separate prior-period perimeter difference. FY2023 D&A also includes the $37.5M spare-satellite write-off. From Q4 2023 estimated satellite lives increased from 12.5 to 17.5 years: FY2023 depreciation fell $27.8M and hosted-payload revenue fell $2.3M from that estimate change. Therefore neither FY2023 EBIT nor the FY2024 D&A step-down is a clean organic operating trend.
