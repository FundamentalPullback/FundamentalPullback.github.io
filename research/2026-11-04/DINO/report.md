## Decision

**Completed; qualifies as a reasonable-price, conditional Stage 3 candidate for a small/partial position, not a cleared Stage 3.5 entry.** The central estimate is **$111** versus the $113.36 close. A 2.3% difference is modeling noise, not a reason to demand an arbitrary discount or to exclude a good-enough price. The separate shareholder cash-flow illustration supports about **11.2% annualized**, close to the assumed 11.5% cost of equity. Diversified cash earnings, a substantial cash balance and a base case that funds known projects make a partial-position interpretation defensible.

That does not make it a compelling full position. A moderately cautious joint case is **$97**, a moderately constructive case **$121**, and the severe commodity-cycle case is **$18**. The price needs several years of elevated refining earnings; immediate normalization gives about $82. The sensible distinction is valuation eligibility versus execution: a small position can be reasonable for an investor accepting cycle volatility, while the actual entry still requires a supported trend/reversal, market/sector checks and a real stop. The November 4 RIN hearing is an additional near-term event, not a reason to mark completed analysis as unfinished.

No universal 15% margin-of-safety rule is applied. There is also no claim that a near-fair-value stock has a positive near-term catalyst or that a small position cannot lose most of its capital under prolonged adverse conditions.

## Business quality and the earnings normalization

Geography, product mix, logistics and brands diversify cash generation more than a pure refiner, but the group still depends heavily on commodity spreads. ## Reproducibility and model conventions

Run `python refiners_model.py` in the companion folder. It regenerates `inputs_and_results.json` and `model_tables.txt`. All amounts in the code are USD billions, volumes are million barrels and share counts are billions. Automated checks require finite values, monotonic margin/claim/WACC behavior and bear < base < bull. No external Python package is needed.

Operating EBITDA = volume × (gross refining margin − refinery cash opex) − refinery SG&A + other consolidated segment EBITDA − corporate cost − incremental RIN stress. EBIT deducts economic D&A. FCFF = EBIT − cash tax + D&A − sustaining cash − growth capex − working-capital investment − special cash costs. SBC remains an expense. Equity-method earnings are excluded from operating EBIT and replaced with cash distributions after JV taxes, debt service and investment, valued at a separate equity discount rate. Owned logistics businesses are included once.

Taxes equal the stated normalized rate on positive EBIT, without immediate tax refunds in loss years. The Q4 bridge starts from September 30 cash/debt, rolls known financing actions, and includes the full unreported quarter; it does not claim to know actual November cash. Q4 is discounted 0.16 years and annual cash flows 1.16–10.16 years. Opening income-tax payables are settled in the stub, rather than assumed to be permanent financing. Working capital is an additional use of $100m in 2027, $50m in 2028 and roughly $25m plus inflation thereafter. These amounts cover normalized growth/inventory needs, not a prediction of volatile quarter-to-quarter oil-price collateral.

Terminal NOPAT grows at g. Terminal net reinvestment is the greater of NOPAT × g/ROIC and any recurring sustaining-capex excess over D&A, with the incremental capex stress included. Terminal FCFF cannot be manufactured by removing turnarounds. The terminal value is floored at zero if continuing operations produce negative terminal cash; this is not a claim that closure is free. Base real physical volume is flat and other-segment EBITDA grows 2% nominally. Cost inflation is 2%. Holding-company debt/finance leases and legacy capital claims are then reconciled to equity. Operating leases are not deducted again because rent remains in operating costs. There is no automatic multiple uplift, no acquisition growth for free and no perpetual subsidy extrapolation.

The November 3 Treasury 10-year yield was **4.95%**. ERP, beta and future borrowing cost are explicit modeling judgments, not claimed live estimates: a 4.5% ERP with beta 1.60 for PBF / 1.45 for DINO implies equity costs of about 12.15% / 11.48%. Assumed pretax debt costs are 7.5% / 6.3%. Current gross financial debt weights of roughly 13% produce WACCs near the selected 11.25% / 10.5%. Sensitivities are more important than small differences in these inputs. ## Regulatory event and execution discipline

The EPA hearing is **November 4, 2026, 9:00 a.m. EST**. The proposal reallocates an additional 770m 2025-exemption RINs either equally across 2026/2027 or entirely into 2027; comments close December 4. A possible compliance-date extension is also proposed. This is not a final rule, a new waiver award, or an assured share-price catalyst. A higher obligation may affect refiners differently depending on exemptions, blending credits, RIN inventories and product-price pass-through. Renewable-fuels interests provide only a partial, imperfect offset. [EPA proposal page](https://www.epa.gov/renewable-fuel-standard/proposed-reallocation-additional-2025-small-refinery-exemptions); [Federal Register proposal and hearing details](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-16/pdf/2026-21155.pdf)

Stage 3 is the completed business/valuation review. Stage 3.5 is a separate execution test: verify broad market and sector conditions, the relevant longer-term trend, a confirmed reversal or support setup, liquidity, event exposure and an actual stop. A fundamental bear value is not a technical stop. A 3:1 trade requires (credible target − entry)/(entry − actual invalidation) ≥ 3, allowing for gaps and execution costs. Neither a bull/base ratio nor a large drawdown from a recent high establishes that trade. No trading action is authorized by this research.

## What would change the conclusion

- Support a partial position: maintained ex-waiver cash margins, ordinary-tax cash conversion, funded closure/JV commitments and an actual technical reversal/stop with sector support. No additional arbitrary price discount is required if those conditions are satisfied.
- Support a larger position: evidence that normalized margins and post-separation cash are better than the central path, or a more attractive price relative to moderate combined downside, with the technical plan still valid.
- Downgrade: margins normalize much faster; RIN costs fail to pass through; lubricants separation loses cash flow or creates more stranded overhead; JV overruns; or apparent cash strength relies on unpaid taxes/compliance.
- Calendar: Nov 4 RIN hearing, later final regulation, planned Q1 2027 Westline FID,2027 lubricants separation and Mississauga cash spending. Earnings-week deferral does not apply because Q3 was releasedOct 28.
