# NEM reserve / project perimeter audit

Primary-source findings as of November 4, 2026. Facts and source URLs are in facts.json. No standalone valuation performed.

## Accounting and reserve perimeter
- NGM is 38.5% proportionately consolidated, not equity accounted. Red Chris is 70% proportionately consolidated. Merian is fully consolidated as a variable-interest entity with 25% Staatsolie NCI (FY25 Note 2). A consolidated operating model therefore includes full Merian cash flows and must remove the outside shareholder's economic claim, rather than blindly equate book NCI with market value.
- Pueblo Viejo (40%) and Lundin Gold (32%, quarterly lag) contribute attributable production, not consolidated mining sales. The latter's Fruta del Norte reserve quantity is not a row in Newmont's P&P reserve table. Keep equity-method cash distributions/values separate and avoid double counting.
- 118.2Moz gold and 12.5Mt copper are attributable P&P CONTAINED metal, before processing loss. They are neither consolidated operating reserves nor recoverable sold volume. Weighted gold/copper metallurgical recoveries are 81%/87%. Site-level recovery is preferable.
- Gold table includes 21.0Moz at undeveloped Wafi-Golpu/NuevaUnion/Norte Abierto and 8.2Moz equity-method Pueblo Viejo. It also includes Red Chris underground reserves requiring the new major project. Removing corporate/non-operating entries leaves about 88.9Moz attributable operating-site reserves from rounded rows, not an issuer-reported total. Full Merian changes that rough current consolidated-site total to about 90.4Moz. This is still not a recoverable reserve total and still includes development-dependent reserves. Do not present the derived numbers as official.
- Copper reserves heavily rely on undeveloped projects: Wafi-Golpu 2.3Mt, NuevaUnion 4.4Mt, Norte Abierto 1.2Mt, versus 12.5Mt total. Treating all as supporting current production indefinitely is untenable.

## Closure and streams
- FY26 closure cash guidance $850m; expected sequential reduction ~$200m in2027 and again2028. Nine-month cash already $714m, of which $531m on Yanacocha water plants. Plants total estimated $1.8bn, $1.3bn spent to date, completion2027. Do not mistake the ~$385m accretion guidance or AISC for this cash requirement.
- September liability $6.773bn includes current $678m and noncurrent $6.095bn; Yanacocha alone $3.387bn reclamation. Future Yanacocha studies can materially increase obligation. Forecast cash or deduct consistent obligation value, not both for identical cash flows.
- Peñasquito streams25% of silver to Wheaton at lesser of market/fixed contract price, escalated up to1.65% annually. Revenue includes noncash acquisition-liability amortization. A cash model must reflect delivery economics and strip noncash amortization; deducting stream book liability again would duplicate the burden.

## Finite-life / resource discipline
Resources (88.1Moz M&I and60.6Moz inferred) are explicitly exclusive of reserves, valued geologically using higher pricing ($2,300 vs standard reserve$2,000), and conversion is uncertain. They require exploration, engineering, recoveries, sustaining/development investment, permits and closure costs. No factual support for free terminal perpetuity. Reserve/production division is only a rough static depletion indicator, not an official mine life: it ignores recovery, sequencing, capex-dependent access and replenishment. The subsequently checked issuer November2026 presentation provides reserve-life indicators by site (facts.json), including3years Yanacocha,8years Peñasquito and2years current RedChris. Those indicators are not annual recoverable production, cost, capital or closure schedules and do not close the NAV requirement.
