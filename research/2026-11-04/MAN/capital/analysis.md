Research date: November 4, 2026. USD millions unless stated. Research only; no target price, recommendation or publication. Numerical inputs and source locators are in facts.json.

## Debt and hedges

Q3 reported debt is 1,103.4: short-term borrowings 35.3, current maturities 505.3 and long-term debt 562.8. Cash is 233.1, giving book net debt 870.3 before other claims. Euro-note **face principal** is EUR400m due June 2027 and EUR500m due December 2030. USD452.5m and USD562.7m are **carrying amounts**, totaling 1,015.2, not independently established USD face principal. Their market fair value is 1,003.1. Debt excluding those carrying amounts is a calculated 88.2. Do not infer spot FX by dividing carrying value by face principal, because carrying amounts can include issuance adjustments. A face-principal USD bridge requires independently sourced valuation-date FX and remaining issue-cost reconciliation.

Q3 derivative assets are 9.2; net-investment swap liabilities are 25.4 current plus 53.5 long term; non-designated forward liabilities are 1.8. Gross liabilities are 80.7 and arithmetic net liabilities 71.5. These balances are separate from notes in the derivative disclosure: **do not add the table's total instrument liabilities 1,095.9 to debt**, because it already includes the 1,015.2 notes.

The 9.2 swap asset hedges CHF intercompany notes. Earnings offsets between those notes and derivatives do not establish that the external derivative is eliminated on consolidation. Net-investment swaps hedge subsidiary investments and contain a significant financing component; future settlement goes through financing cash flow. Neither that hedge designation nor AOCL recognition makes the 78.9 external liability disappear. Conversely, adding 78.9 while ignoring the 9.2 asset is a conservative choice, not the disclosed net balance. Any realizable asset credit needs consistent treatment of corresponding exposure and counterparty/settlement rights. No legal netting conclusion was established.

## Pension, compensation and other claims

FY25 pension recognized liabilities total 113.5, versus separate surplus assets 42.2: aggregate net deficit 71.3. The unfunded retiree healthcare plan adds 8.9. Gross pension plus healthcare claims therefore total 122.4 before supported surplus offsets. These are December 2025 measurements, not refreshed September 2026 deficits. The filing does not establish that the pension surplus is unrestricted or transferable across plans. Do not label 42.2 surplus cash. Q3 expected 2026 pension contributions rose to 21.0, with 13.4 paid through September.

Deferred compensation had matching asset and liability of 188.6 each at FY25. Q3 states asset value 194.1 but does not separately disclose its liability. A zero net treatment is a transparent matching assumption, not newly verified equality. Adding all 194.1 as excess cash is unsupported; deducting a liability without recognizing matched assets would also misstate exposure.

Q3 gross UTBs are 41.6 including interest/penalties: do not add penalties again. More importantly, FY25 separately reports gross UTBs of 41.4 and a commitments-section tax liability of 15.6 including interest/penalties. Thus gross UTBs are not demonstrably incremental cash debt. Q3 cash-liability reconciliation remains open. Treat 41.6 only as gross exposure/sensitivity if used, not a proven bridge deduction.

The 19.0 restructuring reserve is expected mostly paid in 2026. Exited-facility lease liabilities are already carried in short-/long-term operating lease liabilities. The 0.5 YTD lease restructuring charge is offset by 0.5 noncash charges in the reserve roll-forward; it is not evidence of a separate 0.5 remaining cash claim. Avoid counting lease obligations both through the 359.0 lease liability and restructuring charges/reserves. Recurring restructuring needs a consistent earnings and cash convention.

Japan JFTC investigation remains unquantified. A zero forecast means **no quantifiable amount disclosed**, not no risk.

## Taxes, dilution and transition costs

Q3 ETR is 49.8%, YTD 48.7%. French business tax, exceptional surcharge, country mix and unbenefited losses contribute. FY25 business tax was 11.8 gross; its phaseout was delayed three years, while the exceptional surcharge was extended for 2026. These are not interchangeable tax items. A statutory 21% normalized rate is unsupported without a country/earnings and tax-adjustment bridge. Nor is approximately 49% necessarily a perpetual forecast: it is a current reported anchor to stress explicitly.

Current outstanding shares are 46.505570m at October 28; Q3 diluted weighted-average shares are 48.1m, including 1.2m incremental diluted awards. Use the former for current basic market capitalization and a justified forward diluted count for per-share value, rather than silently interchanging denominators.

Prising's 2027 executive-chair package includes salary 1.0, target bonus 140% and target LTI 5.2 through year end. Their combined target package is not a funded claim to deduct wholesale from equity value. It requires future expense/cash/dilution treatment, and the incremental change from prior compensation was not established. The October 28 8-K concerns director Lidia Fonseca, not this CEO package.
