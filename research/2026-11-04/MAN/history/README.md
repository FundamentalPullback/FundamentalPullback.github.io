## Cash flow, payroll and working capital

FCF means reported CFO minus total reported capex, including capitalized owned-software spending. It is not unlevered FCF and includes cash interest/tax, SBC add-back and working-capital timing. No recurring-CFO uplift is assumed. Staffing payroll is generally settled weekly/monthly while receivables remain outstanding longer; growth can consume cash and contraction can temporarily release it. At Sep2026, the nine-month CFO −137.0 includes WC use303.5, principally settlement/collection timing. CFO is not earnings quality proof by itself. Cash-flow WC uses statement changes excluding acquisition/disposition/FX effects; it is not a subtraction of two balance sheets. Asset-increase XBRL signs are explicitly inverted to cash-impact signs. Source CFO bridges and 4Q-to-TTM sums are tested.

FY2025 DSO55 and Jun2026 DSO56 signal collection timing matters. No quantified factoring or receivable-sale schedule was located in the reviewed FY25/Q3 filings. That is a disclosure limitation, **not proof of zero factoring**; no factoring adjustment is assumed. A receivable securitization/transfer reconciliation would be needed before claiming CFO is factoring-neutral. Staffing revenues include the economics of providing labor; do not remove payroll or presumed reimbursements without contract-level principal/agent evidence. FY25 Note1 says agent revenue is net, particularly TAPFIN MSP; sales/VAT collected are excluded. Standalone reimbursed/pass-through labor amounts are not disclosed here, so no invented net-revenue restatement is made.
