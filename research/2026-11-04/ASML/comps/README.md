Run `python calculate.py`. `inputs.json` contains all source URLs and FY/latest-quarter/prior-year-quarter inputs; `numeric_rows.csv` is a portable, source-linked numeric fact extract. `results.json` holds calculated outputs. Raw HTML and full extracted TXT filings are local working material only: do not publish or attach them. Publish only original analytical text and necessary numeric facts with links.

TTM = FY June 2026 + September 2026 quarter - September 2025 quarter. KLAC year ends June 30; Lam uses fiscal weeks (June 28, September 27, September 28 prior year). Latest fiscal quarters/TTM are close to ASML September 2026 but not literally identical periods; don't apply these multiples as an ASML price target. Both are adjacent wafer-fab-equipment comparables, not lithography substitutes.

EBIT is consolidated GAAP operating income: KLAC sales minus COGS, R&D and SG&A (zero FY26 impairment), excluding other income and interest. Lam directly reports operating income. Not non-GAAP adjusted EBIT or EBIT including investment income. CFO and capex are cash-flow statement figures. Lam capex includes intangible purchases. Owner FCF is CFO - capex - SBC, an equity cash-flow proxy; yield denominator is market capitalization, not enterprise value. It retains working-capital swings, cash interest and cash taxes. It is not normalized free cash flow or a forward estimate. KLAC TTM CFO and capex independently tie to the release's direct twelve-month figures.
