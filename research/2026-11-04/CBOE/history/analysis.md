# Cboe Global Markets financial history

As of November 4, 2026. USD millions except per-share data. Source-reported GAAP income and cash-flow statements are retained separately from analyst calculations. The latest 10-Q and earnings release were filed October 30, 2026.

## What matters

- TTM net revenue reached $2,843.8m, up 24.59%; GAAP EBIT rose 40.97% to $1,919.7m. The latter includes the $71.5m Australia disposal gain. Conservative normalized EBIT is $1,874.7m, up 35.51%.
- Reported TTM CFO of $2,626.0m includes $1,394.5m of clearing/customer-fund inflows. Removing that specific movement and deducting full cash capex produces $1,135.1m, down 1.17%, rather than the misleading $2,529.6m reported-CFO-minus-capex result.
- This ex-clearing cash measure still contains regulatory fee timing, working-capital changes, and tax settlements. It is a cash-generation proxy, not unrestricted or normalized free cash flow. TTM cash taxes were $864.4m versus $331.9m. No unsupported tax addback is made.
- Corporate cash plus unmatched financial investments, excluding Canada held-for-sale cash, is $2,663.1m. Matched clearing/customer funds, deferred-compensation assets, regulatory/operating needs and foreign cash restrictions require separate treatment. Face debt is $1,450.0m, including $650m due January 2027.

Conservative EBIT = GAAP EBIT − disposal gains + disclosed impairments + acquisition contingent-consideration remeasurements. FY2023 thus removes a $14.4m contingent-consideration gain. It retains acquisition expenses and all SBC/amortization.

Broader exit-cost sensitivity additionally reverses identified strategic realignment costs after removing impairment overlap, plus the small digital syndication adjustment. TTM conservative EBIT $1,874.7m plus $48.1m additional exit costs yields $1,922.8m. This is a sensitivity because realignment costs have recurred across years.

The $14.0m CAT-note collectability reserve is retained in both base proxies because collection risk is an economic cost. A separately named sensitivity adds it back. CEO sign-on awards are SBC and are never removed. Company-adjusted EBIT is available separately and should not be confused with these stricter measures.

No analyst-normalized EPS is supplied. The Australia gain is described as non-taxable, but allocation to participating securities and tax consequences of other adjustments are not sufficiently separated to justify a manufactured normalized EPS.

## Disposals and comparability

Australia closed July 31, 2026. The $71.5m gain is removed from normalized EBIT. Canada remains held for sale and pending approvals at the October 30 reporting date; its tax consequences cannot yet be reasonably estimated. The roughly $300m combined price is not incremental cash to add again after Australia proceeds already arrived.

Canada assets held for sale of $169.6m and liabilities of $10.5m include $57.4m cash. These are not discontinued operations. Depreciation/amortization of held-for-sale assets ceased in April 2026, so even the historical continuing-scope EBIT is not a clean constant-asset-basis run rate.

The organic reconciliation discloses Australia net revenue of $20.3m in 9M2026 and $21.5m in 9M2025. Exact FY2025/Q42025 Australia net revenue and standalone current Canada P&L were not located in these SEC filings; no full pro-forma TTM bridge is fabricated. TMX buyer combined FY2025 figures of approximately C$87m revenue and C$25m adjusted EBITDA use unaudited IFRS/Australian standards and are not inserted into Cboe US GAAP history.

Historical consolidated net-revenue totals are comparable under the FY2025 financial-statement revenue captions. Digital ceased being a separate segment in 2025; individual-segment history requires its own bridge. Regulatory-cost labeling changed from Section 31 fees to regulatory fees cost of revenues, without changing the historical totals used here.
