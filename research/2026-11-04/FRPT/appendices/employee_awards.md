All 927,000 service options were exercisable, with the same $50.09 weighted exercise price. Performance options exercisable were 1,351,000 at $80.10, with 3.1 years remaining. In-the-money exercisable counts were 622,000 service and 679,000 performance options; aggregate intrinsic values were $30.414m and $29.503m respectively, at year-end prices. No options were granted in 2024 or 2025. Annual Note 12 also reports unrecognized service-RSU compensation of $27.735m, performance-RSU compensation of $3.932m for probable vesting, and market-RSU compensation of $1.116m.

**H1 2026 changes, not a complete rollforward:** no option grants; 534,880 options exercised. Service RSUs granted were 191,956 at weighted grant-date fair value $80.95, including 7,500 CFO inducement units; 152,124 RSUs vested. No performance RSUs were granted. Market/TSR units granted were 46,344 target units at $91.94 weighted grant-date fair value. These vest after the performance period ending December 31, 2028, with delivery ranging from zero to 2.5 times target. The October 23 filing separately reports 58,245 Baty retention RSUs vesting June 30, 2029.

The Q2 diluted-EPS table adds 243,000 service-option, 116,000 RSU, 479,000 performance-option and 5,776,000 convertible shares. H1 increments are 354,000, 164,000, 503,000 and 5,776,000 respectively. These are period-specific treasury-stock/if-converted calculations, not the outstanding award pool and not an economic capped-call-adjusted share bridge.

**Model controls:** refresh common shares separately after Q3. Do not add all GAAP diluted shares to current common shares. Do not subtract all convertible debt and simultaneously assume gross physical conversion without reconciling settlement. Use existing employee award dilution separately, with strike/vesting assumptions explicit. The disclosed weighted-average strikes do not permit an exact current tranche-level calculation. Do not assume all year-end awards remain outstanding after H1 exercises, or that grants equal actual future payout. Ordinary recurring SBC remains an operating cost; this inventory addresses dilution, not an automatic expense add-back. No capitalized SBC is inferred from equity-accounting differences.
