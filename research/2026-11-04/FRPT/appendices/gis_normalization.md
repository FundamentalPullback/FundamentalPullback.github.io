## Nature of adjustments

- **Noncash impairments:** FY26 $1,500.0m North America Pet goodwill plus $302.9m indefinite-lived brand impairments. The latter consists of $250.0m Nudges/True Chews and $52.9m Uncle Toby’s. The pet goodwill impairment was not tax-deductible. The Blue Buffalo brand itself was described as having risk of decreasing valuation coverage, not as the specific brand written down. Impairments are economically informative even if removed from a single-period operating comparison.
- **Held-for-sale valuation losses:** FY26 $1,031.8m and Q1 FY27 $23.7m are expressly noncash pretax losses for the planned Brazil divestiture. They are distinct from restructuring cash payments and from gains on completed divestitures. Do not call all $2.971bn on the FY26 expense line cash restructuring.
- **Divestiture gains:** FY26 net gain $1,049.4m, compared with Q1 FY26 gain $1,054.4m. After the prior quarter rolls out, the mechanical trailing reconciliation adds $5.0m, representing a residual net loss, not a continuing billion-dollar gain. These gains sit above GAAP operating profit in this issuer’s presentation.
- **Restructuring and transformation:** FY26 charges $155.5m included $95.4m new supply-chain actions and $60.1m earlier actions. The new plan recorded $71.0m asset write-offs and $24.4m other costs in FY26; projected total charges were about $101m, of which about $33m cash. The firm paid net restructuring cash of $92.5m in FY26, $21.0m in Q1 FY26, and $2.9m in Q1 FY27, producing $74.4m over the mechanical trailing period. These cash payments do not equal current charges or the severance-reserve utilization line. Repeated multi-year programs should not be automatically treated as costless or permanently excluded.
- **Classification:** FY26 total restructuring, transformation and impairment charges of $2,990.2m split between $2,970.8m on the separately shown expense line and $19.4m in cost of sales. Q1 FY27 totals of $23.9m split $21.4m and $2.5m. The issuer bridge separates valuation loss from ordinary restructuring within those totals.
- **Commodity mark-to-market:** removes timing changes on derivative positions and certain grain inventories, not the eventual economic input cost. The quarterly note explains that amounts are allocated to segment costs as underlying exposures affect earnings. Do not erase actual commodity cost or treat all volatility as irrelevant.
- **Transaction/integration and investment items:** retain separately for judgment. They have real costs and can recur in an acquisitive business. Do not automatically adopt all issuer exclusions.
- **SBC and amortization:** ordinary SBC remains in GAAP and issuer adjusted operating profit. No ordinary finite-lived intangible amortization add-back appears in this bridge. The latest note estimates approximately $19m annual finite-lived intangible amortization for each of the next five years; this is an estimate, not the measured current expense. Accelerated depreciation can sit within restructuring. If that charge is excluded from EBIT, the D&A add-back must be reconciled to avoid counting it twice. Pension non-service income and the $2m early debt-redemption loss are below operating profit and should not be added back to EBIT again.
