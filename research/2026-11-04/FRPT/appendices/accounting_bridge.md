**Established:** each figure is present in the primary filing. FY2023–24 differences exactly equal total finance-lease amortization. FY2025's EBITDA D&A also equals gross-profit-reconciliation depreciation 61.426 + SG&A-reconciliation D&A 25.446 = 86.872. H1 2026 equals 35.156 + 13.374 = 48.530. These are not decimal/scanner extraction mistakes.

**Strong but unconfirmed inference:** FY2025/H1 2026 differences correspond to amortization of the original equipment finance lease, leaving that charge inside issuer EBITDA. The legacy gross ROU asset was 28.478 at FY2024 end, with a ten-year equipment agreement (annual straight-line approximate 2.848). A land finance lease was added in 2025, and total gross ROU became 35.714. This explains why total finance-lease amortization now exceeds the D&A bridge difference. The reviewed issuer text does not provide an explicit line-by-line bridge, so do not present this inference as management-confirmed accounting policy.

**Actionable control:** preserve two D&A columns. For GAAP EBIT-to-FCFF, use total GAAP operating D&A and handle finance leases consistently as debt or as operating cash costs. For reproducing issuer adjusted EBITDA, use its published D&A. If finance lease depreciation is added back to FCFF and liability is subtracted in the EV bridge, future asset replacement/lease investment must be represented; do not take perpetual free financing. Never force normalized EBIT from EBITDA with the wrong D&A.

SBC note differences are separately explicable: FY2024 employee expense 49.779 + supplier-warrant amortization 2.027 ≈ 51.807 total (rounding). FY2023 employee 19.774 + supplier warrants 5.160 ≈ 24.935. FY2025 supplier warrants zero; expense 13.883 was reduced by performance reversals. Equity statement recognition is not the same as expense and is not a safe proxy for PP&E-capitalized SBC.
