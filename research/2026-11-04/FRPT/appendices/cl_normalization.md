## What each adjustment means
- Impairment: FY2025 fourth-quarter skin-health/primarily Filorga noncash write-downs: goodwill 582, trademark 244, customer relationships 93, total 919. These impairments are separate from ordinary D&A. Source: FY2025 10-K MD&A page 32 “Goodwill and Intangible Assets Impairment Charges,” fy2025.txt lines 2670–2671; Note 5. They remain fully in TTM GAAP results.
- Restructuring: FY2025 operating expense 13 = SG&A 6 + Other expense 7. FY2025 current Strategic Growth and Productivity Program cash payments were 3, with year-end accrual 10. A separate prior 2022 program paid 17 in cash in 2025 despite no new 2025 expense. Source: FY2025 MD&A pages 44–46, fy2025.txt lines 3182–3315; Note 4.
- 9M2026 restructuring: total pretax charges 333, but only 327 in operating profit (cost of sales 7, SG&A 20, Other expense 300). The remaining 6 is non-service postretirement cost below operating profit and must NOT be added to this EBIT bridge. Charges consisted of employee-related 294 and asset-related/other 39; SGPP cash payments 140, charges against assets/liabilities 11, FX -20, closing accrual 172. Employee-related asset/liability charges include the 6 postretirement liability increase, so the whole 11 is not simply accelerated depreciation. Q3 pretax charges 30, operating charges 29, cash payments 76. Source: 10-Q MD&A pages 47–49, cl_q326.txt lines 4971–5159; Note 4.
- Restructuring cash risk: latest issuer estimate is 80%–90% of the full program's 350–550 pretax charges will produce cash expenditures through 2028. Thus an operating-profit addback does not eliminate the cash burden. Do not use accounting charge 327 as cash restructuring paid or add it to CFO. A precise all-program TTM cash-restructuring total is not claimed here.
- ERISA: operating-only adjustments are 99 FY2025 and 15 9M2025, leaving 84 in TTM. Below-operating postretirement ERISA amounts differ and should not enter the operating-profit bridge. FY2025 MD&A page 34 describes reclassification of settlement-related attorneys' fees to SG&A, helping explain the period difference.
- Acquisition costs: 9 FY2025 and 9 9M2025, so zero net TTM adjustment. This is distinct from ordinary acquired-intangible amortization.
- Ordinary amortization: the issuer does not exclude ordinary intangible amortization in these operating-profit reconciliations. FY2025 intangible amortization of 78 is explicitly retained in non-GAAP Other expense. Source: FY2025 MD&A page 32 and Note 17, fy2025.txt lines 2645–2669 and 8363–8418. No ordinary amortization addback is made.
- Ordinary SBC: not excluded in these reconciliations. TTM SBC 169 remains an operating cost. Do not add it back again while labeling the result issuer-adjusted operating profit.
- Divestiture gains: no separate divestiture gain adjustment appears in these issuer operating-profit bridges. FY2025 disclosed 1 of net losses from marketable securities and sale of other assets, retained in non-GAAP Other expense; this combined line is not a separately identified divestiture gain. No invented subtraction or zero-underlying-gain claim is made.
