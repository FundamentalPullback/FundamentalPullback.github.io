## NCI and old subsidiary preferred interests

CL's $352m is **book equity, not verified market value**. Q3 10-Q consolidated balance sheet, page 4, compares it with $311m at December 31, 2025. The consolidated income statement reports NCI-attributable net income of $91m for 9M2026 and $97m for 9M2025; FY2025 was $129m. These are evidence that the claim is real and income-producing, not a basis to invent a market value. For an approximate consolidated EV comparison, identify a book-NCI proxy explicitly if used, or disclose its omission; do not call $352m a market value.

GIS's $13.4m is likewise book equity. Q1 FY2027 balance sheet, page 6, compares $12.2m at May 31, 2026. The much larger historical General Mills Cereals LLC Class A minority interest with a floating preferred return is no longer outstanding: FY2026 10-K Note 10 says GIS purchased it in Q4 FY2025 for $252.8m, comprising the $242.3m original capital balance and $10.5m mainly capital appreciation. Do not add an obsolete $242.3m or $252.8m preferred/NCI claim.

CHWY Q2 Note 2, page 8, describes Chewy and its wholly-owned subsidiaries. Note 3, pages 11–12, says SmartPak and Modern Animal acquisitions were 100%. There is no reported NCI or redeemable-NCI balance or earnings attribution. This supports no reported NCI adjustment, rather than an independently measured zero market value. Preferred stock is expressly zero outstanding at August 2 and February 1, 2026, with 5m shares authorized.

## Lease and capital consistency

CL FY2025 Note 7 includes finance leases in the debt table, even though Note 15 calls them immaterial. Both can be true: disclosed $72m is small relative to consolidated debt. Current $7,716m debt is the latest balance-sheet carrying sum; do not append the stale $72m separately. The latest finance-lease split is unknown.

GIS FY2026 Note 9 labels the reconciliation line as the net impact of discounts, issuance costs, swaps **and finance leases**, so a separate addition of the older $0.4m risks duplication. The Q1 $13,615.6m compact debt sum also includes junior subordinated notes; these are not an extra preferred-equity claim. No rating-agency equity treatment has been imposed. The exact current finance-lease component remains undisclosed in the reviewed Q1 note.

CHWY's term-loan principal/carrying distinction is established. Q2 Note 9, page 17, discusses equipment finance leases but discloses only operating-lease balances in its table. FY2025 Note 8, page 69, explicitly calls finance leases at February 1, 2026 and February 2, 2025 immaterial, and the cash-flow statement shows $0.2m FY2025 finance-lease principal repayment. Do not invent a current amount, assert zero, or claim it is inside term-loan debt. Treat it as a small historical but currently unquantified limitation.

Operating leases are a separate convention: do not append operating-lease liabilities to EV without aligning the earnings denominator. Existing packet records the available dated balances. No additional material preferred or redeemable-NCI claim was identified in these local filings.

## FRPT pullback causality

No exact issuer/SEC statement attributing the stock pullback to a specific cause, or a verified dated price-event chronology, was established in the existing FRPT notes. The Q2/Q3 materials document competition, household growth, inflation and guidance changes, but these do not prove why the stock moved. The scanner price/decline is not primary evidence of causality. Do not assign a cause from these operating facts alone.
