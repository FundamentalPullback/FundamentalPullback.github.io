**Added November 4, 2026. All figures in these tables are USD millions.** This supplement provides the complete numeric history behind the valuation. Annual statements are audited; interim statements are unaudited. Fiscal dates are stated explicitly. Quarterly cash flows are derived from successive year-to-date statements, and Q4 is the full year less the first nine months. These are calculations, not company-reported standalone cash-flow statements.

Negative figures are cash uses. Connected-device and commission figures are **net increases in capitalized balances**, not gross purchases. Their amortization is already in operating costs; adding all of it back as ordinary PP&E D&A while subtracting only these net changes would overstate cash generation. Deferred revenue provided less financing in the current period despite faster growth. The arbitration receivable offsets the noncash gain in CFO, so the gain must not be subtracted from cash FCF again.

A separate operating-only diagnostic starts from normalized EBIT, applies an illustrative 26% steady-state tax only to positive profit, adds eligible PP&E D&A, and deducts cash capex, capitalized software compensation and capex accrual changes. Before incremental operating capital this is approximately **−0.417 current versus −128.351 prior**. Including the historical operating-capital changes, but excluding the non-operating arbitration receivable, gives approximately **−129.749 versus −160.249**. These are transparent analyst normalizations, not issuer guidance, GAAP measures or substitutes for the model's explicit NOL schedule. Current growth requires real device/customer-acquisition funding; the large difference from cash FCF is therefore economically meaningful rather than an arithmetic error.

**Valuation consequence:** the evidence supports the existing improving-business / WAIT-on-price conclusion, with no mechanical change to the $18.36 base value. The current TTM cash capex plus disclosed software compensation and capex accrual increase is about 44.595m. The forecast's 2.3% economic-capex allowance on FY2027 guided sales of 2.045bn is about 47.035m and already budgets capitalization; charging the same compensation again would double count. The future 15% incremental operating-capital assumption is still a favorable scale/timing normalization, not a description of the more demanding latest cash record. Margin and cash-conversion confidence should remain lower than revenue-history confidence.
