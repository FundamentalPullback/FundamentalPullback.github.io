## Distribution and ownership are not uniform
The elected structure is a split-off. Accepted tenders redeem Eaton ordinary shares in return for newly issued SpinCo shares, reducing Eaton's outstanding denominator. If undersubscribed, only the remaining SpinCo shares are distributed pro rata to Eaton shares still outstanding. Accepted tendered shares waive rights to that clean-up distribution; rejected or untendered shares remain eligible. Fully subscribed exchange leaves no clean-up entitlement for nonparticipants. Former Eaton holders collectively receive 50.1% of the combined fully diluted shares, not an automatic uniform allocation to every current ETN share.

The preliminary discount, upper exchange-ratio limit, indicative ratio, valuation dates, expiration and clean-up record date are not finalized. There is no minimum exchange ratio. Pricing references three trading-day VWAP averages ending on the second full trading day before expiration; prices can move after ratio fixation. Oversubscription invokes proration. Beneficial holders of fewer than 100 shares tendering all may qualify for the odd-lot exemption, excluding Eaton 401(k) shares. Tenders can be withdrawn before expiration. The pro forma estimated 98,079,878 SpinCo shares for Eaton holders and Dana one-for-one merger conversion subject to adjustment are not a finalized entitlement per ETN share. See questions and answers and pro forma EPS discussion.

## Cash routing and financing
The approximately $1.1 billion headline is not fixed net cash to the listed parent. It is adjusted for cash/debt, distribution true-up, preclosing Dana buybacks from 27 July through the day before closing multiplied by 100.4008016%, and separate Royal Precision and Specified Assets purchase routes. Taxes, fees and jurisdictional routing matter. Pro forma page 153 shows beginning 1,101 + buyback adjustment 301 - Royal Precision 245 - Specified Assets 363 = SpinCo cash payment 794, USD millions. The 245 and 363 are separate direct purchases from Eaton entities; they are not lost economic proceeds. Do not simply replace aggregate Eaton proceeds with 794 or subtract those amounts without also reconciling the direct receipts. None of these estimates certifies closing net distributable cash.

Original bridge commitment 2,600 was reduced by 350 when Term A 350 was committed: remaining bridge 2,250 plus Term A 350 still totals 2,600, not 2,950. Revolver 1,200 is expected undrawn and is not cash. Pro forma financing cash bridge is 2,600 proceeds - 1,111 Dana refinancing - 794 SpinCo cash payment - 28 cash financing costs - 300 Dana buybacks = +367. Net book debt adjustment is 1,468, not the gross proceeds, because existing debt is refinanced and issuance costs change. These are June 30 pro forma balances, not current ETN debt or funds already drawn.

Bridge maturity is 364 days after distribution; illustrative rate starts SOFR +2.75%, with +0.50% at 90,180,270 days. Pro forma uses June30 SOFR3.68% and full-step 7.93% interest assumption. Term A and revolver start SOFR +1.375%, leverage-grid adjustments and five-year maturity. These assumptions and potential permanent refinancing are not guaranteed final financing. Merger agreement says obligations are not conditioned upon obtaining financing; this does not guarantee funding or remove financing risk. Distribution separately requires payment and solvency conditions.

## Timing and closing conditions
Updated expected completion is the first four months of 2027, not a firm date or confirmed Q1. Outside date is June10,2027, with a conditional three-month extension for outstanding regulatory approvals or restructuring/distribution conditions. Dana stockholder approval, regulatory clearances, effective registration statements without stop order, NYSE listing, absence of legal prohibition, restructuring/distribution, tax opinions, representations/covenants and other conditions remain. Distribution also requires sufficient Eaton distributable reserves, SpinCo cash payment and independent solvency/surplus opinions. Do not label filed registration effective or conditions satisfied from this review. $158.7 million Dana termination fee applies only to specified circumstances, not certain failure proceeds. Tax-free treatment is intended, not blanket guaranteed tax exemption, and direct asset sales/global taxes are distinct.

## Valuation consequence
A DCF of consolidated current Eaton including Mobility and without pending COL is a clearly labeled counterfactual operating perimeter, not certified current shareholder total value. Do not also add the $1.1 billion or 50.1% combined equity, or reduce ETN shares without exchange terms. Closing analysis needs retained-Eaton standalone flows, stranded costs, taxes/net cash, SpinCo combined equity after correctly reconciled financing, allocation of share rights, and final post-exchange denominator. Even failed RMT is not an indefinite unchanged-perimeter base because Eaton says it intends a standalone spin-off if RMT is not consummated. Keep total valuation null until those bridges are resolved; component scenarios may be useful with explicit conditions.
