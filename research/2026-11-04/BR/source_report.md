# Broadridge (BR): completed Stage 3 remediation and model revision

**4 November 2026, before the US market opens. USD millions except per-share values.** The November 3 Q1 FY2027 release and 10-Q are incorporated. Reference **$183.76** is both the November 3 scanner price and verified regular-session NYSE close, not the $185.45 after-hours quotation. This revision supersedes the analytical conclusion and model framing of A154; the earlier source report remains historical. No trade is approved.

## Decision

**Keep on the conditional recovery watchlist; do not treat the old $124.33 estimate as the sole defensible valuation.** Revised organic-core bear/base/bull values are **$87.09 / $153.27 / $240.38**, with a same-discount-rate recovery case of **$184.50**. The current price is about 19.9% above the base, but close to a credible stronger operating path. That is a meaningful change from saying only implausible single-variable growth or margins can support the price.

At $183.76, the central case does not provide a positive valuation cushion. A purchase would require explicitly underwriting the recovery path, rather than relabeling it as the base or claiming that any bullish scenario authorizes a partial position. Current research disposition is **observation / conditional recovery, not a completed entry approval**. A good technical setup cannot by itself prove the stronger fundamental path. Conversely, absence of 15% growth or a 15/20/25% discount is not a universal rejection rule. Stage 3.5 and a portfolio decision remain separate.

Confidence is high in the reported arithmetic, moderate in the recurring franchise, and lower in the precise margin/reinvestment path. The scenario span is not a statistical confidence interval, stop-loss range, or promised target.

## What the audit repaired

1. **Original model reproduced:** $70.187 / $124.330 / $212.645, matching A154. It was not a spreadsheet arithmetic error.
2. **Discount rate calibrated:** Treasury, ERP, beta, debt assumption and capital weights are now explicit. Keeping original cash flows but using the calibrated WACC raises $124.33 to $131.82.
3. **Capital claims reconciled:** the original bridge subtracted gross retirement obligations net only of Rabbi Trust assets, while giving all other disclosed investments zero credit. The revised bridge includes the full investment pool once, against gross obligations, and adds a separately identified past-tax reserve. Same old cash flows after this bridge give $133.99.
4. **Reinvestment and acquired amortization separated:** the old explicit forecast combined a 4.5% investment budget with persistent acquired-amortization economics. That can be a conservative purchased-growth case, but should not be the only model for an organic growth thesis. The revised core keeps operating technology/client investment, runs off existing purchase-accounting amortization, and funds terminal growth. This produces $153.27 rather than hiding a conservative replacement-acquisition assumption inside a universal valuation rule.
5. **Current peers and joint reverse cases added:** the earlier report explicitly lacked fresh SS&C/FactSet multiples. The comparison below uses November 3 closes and latest reported cash/debt, not stale vendor ratio screens.

## Business, latest results and normalization needs

Broadridge has recurring financial-market infrastructure, regulatory communications and client workflows with meaningful switching costs. Its scale and relationships are valuable; cyber incidents, outages, regulatory changes and implementation failures can still damage that franchise. Distribution/postage revenue is low-margin pass-through activity, and event-driven proxy revenue is volatile. Consolidated revenue growth is therefore not the same as recurring-fee growth.

Q1 FY2027 recurring revenue grew 11%, about 9% organically; total revenue grew 5%. Event-driven revenue fell 41%. Adjusted operating profit fell 9% even though adjusted EPS increased 3%, partly because of share repurchases. Q1 total EBIT was 173.5 versus 188.8. Management retained 6–8% constant-currency recurring growth, approximately 21% adjusted operating margin, approximately 19% GAAP operating margin, 8–12% adjusted EPS growth and 100%+ free-cash-flow conversion guidance. The capex/software amount in the cash-conversion reconciliation is approximately 123. [S1–S3]

The quarter's 13.6% tax rate benefits from transferable federal credits related to the prior year. The cash-flow footnote includes amounts paid to third parties for purchased tax credits in cash taxes; adding them again would double count. We use 25% normalized tax, not the exceptional quarterly rate. The prior and current earnings comparisons also contain noncash Canton/CNTN gains and losses, treated separately below.

The operating recovery is not based solely on the 9% organic headline. GTO capital-markets growth included a 4-point benefit from software term licenses; wealth growth included a 3-point license benefit. These are real sales but their timing is not automatically a perpetual growth rate. Distribution growth largely reflected postage increases. A durable recovery needs repeatable recurring-fee growth and conversion into operating cash, not token gains or licensing timing alone. [S2]

## Catalysts, evidence that changes the view and technical boundary

November 3 results are complete; BR is not deferred as an upcoming earnings event. No next issuer-announced earnings date was verified. Watch recurring-fee growth excluding term-license timing, closed sales conversion, ICS event normalization, expansion of operating margin without cutting needed platform/client investment, and after-tax economic cash growth. Digital token value is a separate balance-sheet sensitivity, not the operating catalyst needed to support $183.76.

Reclassification toward a reasonable-price core candidate would require evidence supporting the recovery economics or a lower price that aligns with the ordinary base. There is no universal discount trigger. Evidence of sustained mid/high-single-digit total growth with margin expansion could justify a higher base; weaker recurring growth, structurally lower event revenue, cyber/regulatory cost or consistently heavier required investment would move it lower. Buying back stock does not by itself create revenue or economic earnings.

The bear value is not a technical stop. Bull upside/adverse-value downside is about 0.59:1, a scenario asymmetry diagnostic rather than a chart reward/risk test. No verified current entry, invalidation price, volume condition, concentration check or execution plan is supplied.
