FORMULAS / DEFINITION
TTM = FY2025 + YTD2026 - YTD2025; MAT YTD = six months; JAKK YTD = nine months.
Owner cash = CFO - capex - SBC. Owner cash yield = owner cash / (price * latest-quarter diluted weighted-average shares).
Equity proxy = price * shares. EV proxy = equity proxy + nominal financial debt - unrestricted cash. Operating leases excluded consistently with EBIT after rent. Weighted-average diluted shares are an explicit proxy, not exact spot shares or full economic dilution.
