# Knowles: portable historical financials
As of 2026-11-04. Run `python rebuild_history.py` offline with Python 3 standard library. No network, packages, browser or original filings required. Outputs: history.json/csv, ledger.csv and qa.txt. USD millions except diluted EPS in USD/share, diluted shares in millions and percentages.

Coverage: FY2023–25; Q4 2024 through Q3 2026 (four latest quarters plus prior-year comparisons); TTM September 2025 and 2026. Raw quarter cash flows are derived from successive year-to-date statements; Q4 additive flows are FY less 9M. TTM is prior FY + current 9M − prior 9M. Current balance-sheet values are snapshots, not period sums. Direct numeric facts retain filing URLs, XBRL tags and dates; manual MD&A inputs retain source/section. `prepare_inputs.py` documents extraction provenance but is not needed for offline rebuild. Full SEC filings are deliberately outside this folder and must not be included in a public bundle.

## Perimeter and interpretation
Consumer MEMS divestiture in December 2024 means revenue, EBIT and continuing NI/EPS use the retrospectively presented continuing perimeter, while GAAP consolidated CFO, capex, SBC and D&A include discontinued operations. Never compare consolidated CFO with continuing net income as an unqualified conversion ratio.

The separate `cfo_continuing_nongaap`, `capex_continuing_nongaap`, and `fcf_continuing_company` series reproduce the company's MD&A reconciliation. Signed discontinued CFO adjustments are explicit; in 2023–24 discontinued operations supplied cash, while 2025–26 used cash. These are perimeter adjustments, not free addbacks or evidence that remaining divestiture/Syntiant obligations can be ignored. Retain consolidated cash generation as an economic constraint. `fcf_continuing_after_sbc_proxy` deducts continuing SBC and is an analytical proxy, not owner earnings or a company-reported measure. Maintenance/growth capex, normalized working capital and cash cost of dilution are not determined here.

FY2025 10-K tags consolidated CFO as `NetCashProvidedByUsedInContinuingOperations`; face cash-flow statement and explicit MD&A reconciliation show 114.0/130.1/122.7 for 2025/24/23 are consolidated, versus non-GAAP continuing 146.5/105.7/79.1. Extraction corrects the semantic interpretation, not the reported numbers.

Purchased software is included in PP&E under the annual accounting policy. Cash software expenditure is not separately identified in reviewed cash-flow statements: `software_capex_separate` is null, NOT zero. Do not add a guessed software amount to reported PP&E capex or assume PP&E excludes it. Continuing depreciation and amortization are separately retained; consolidated D&A has a wider historical perimeter.

Q4 diluted EPS and weighted shares are directly sourced from the February 5, 2026 earnings release, including its Q4 2024 comparative; the latter was also checked against the February 13, 2025 release. Q4 2025 diluted EPS continuing/total = 0.29/0.24; Q4 2024 = 0.12/0.21. TTM reported EPS and weighted shares remain null. Separate *_sum_quarters EPS fields are calculated sums of four directly reported quarterly diluted EPS, not a GAAP-reported TTM measure, and may differ from an annualized denominator computation. Annual diluted EPS less 9M EPS is not valid Q4 diluted EPS because denominators and anti-dilution can change. FY/9M/quarter data use newest available comparative filings, prioritized by actual filing date.

2026 current debt is derived as debt-note total less noncurrent debt (both describe the sole revolver at those dates). This derivation is restricted to 2026 because the similarly named 2024 note fact excludes current term-loan maturities. Liabilities are assets minus stockholders' equity. Net debt uses carrying debt excluding operating leases, not enterprise-value adjustments.

## Checks
Rebuild verifies continuing CFO bridge, continuing FCF bridge, both TTM/four-quarter additive reconciliations where available, and null EPS/unknown software handling. qa.txt lists each result. checksums.txt permits deterministic comparison. The scripts verify accounting identities, not completeness of every possible disclosure or economic normalization.
