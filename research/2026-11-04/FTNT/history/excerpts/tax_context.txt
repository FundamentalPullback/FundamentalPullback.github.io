Section: Management discussion, Provision for income taxes
Our effective tax rate was 16% for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to an effective tax rate of 20% for the same period last year. The decrease in our effective tax rate compared to the same period in 2025 was primarily due to an increase in the FDDEI deduction and an increase in excess tax benefits from stock-based compensation. The provision for income taxes for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily comprised of U.S. federal and state taxes, withholding taxes and foreign taxes totaling $181.2 million, which was favorably affected by a tax benefit of $36.2 million from the FDDEI deduction and excess tax benefits from stock-based compensation expense of $25.5 million. 
The provision for income taxes for the three months ended September 30, 2025 was primarily comprised of U.S. federal and state taxes, withholding taxes, and foreign taxes totaling $141.0 million, which was favorably affected by a tax benefit of $11.1 million from the FDDEI deduction and excess tax benefits from stock-based compensation expense of $9.2 million.
Our effective tax rate was 17% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to an effective tax rate of 18% for the same period last year. The provision for income taxes for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily comprised of U.S. federal and state taxes, withholding taxes and foreign taxes totaling $513.1 million, which was favorably affected by a tax benefit of $104.1 million from the FDDEI deduction and excess tax benefits from stock-based compensation expense of $52.5 million.
The provision for income taxes for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 was primarily comprised of U.S. federal and state taxes, withholding taxes and foreign taxes totaling $384.2 million as well as a tax provision of $30.6 million related to the derecognition of deferred tax assets from the business combination with Linksys. This provision for income taxes was favorably affected by a tax benefit of $62.0 million from the FDDEI deduction and excess tax benefits from stock-based compensation expense of $58.5 million.
