## Convertible notes and hedge: use one coherent method
Original principal $1,250 less surrendered principal $153 implies $1,097 remaining; filing text rounds remaining principal to $1.1bn. Balance-sheet fair-value debt is $1,774, and the separate capped-call asset is $153. One fair-value bridge deducts net $1,621 and adds no further conversion dilution for that same claim. These are July-date market values, not freshly revalued November figures.

Alternative structural method: deduct principal $1,097 and separately value only the residual conversion claim net of capped calls. Per $1,000 principal, conversion consideration is 4.3161 PANW shares plus $88.263 cash. Principal itself is cash-settled; any excess is issuer-elected cash or cash/shares. Effective strike is (1000−88.263)/4.3161 = approximately $211.24. Total conversion shares underlying remaining notes are approximately 4.7347617m, but these are NOT all incremental shares on top of cash repayment of principal. At price P, unhedged conversion premium in $m is max(0, 4.7347617×P + 96.824511−1097). Divide this premium by P only if modeling share settlement of that excess. Actual corporate-event adjustments and early conversion conditions matter.

Capped calls strike near $211.24 with caps $287.21–291.44. Approximate terminal hedge protection is underlying shares × max(0,min(P,cap)−strike), bounded by the cap range because tranche weighting was not established. This is a maturity-payoff illustration, not an option fair-value model. Do not additionally add the $153 fair-value hedge asset if its payoff is already netted against conversion exposure.

FY26 note fair-value earnings loss $620 less capped-call gain $58 = net nonoperating loss $562. Do not treat it as recurring operating expense. Old 2025 warrants were fully net-settled in FY26, issuing 27m shares already embedded in current outstanding shares; incremental old-warrant claim is zero.

## Deferred compensation: liability is not synonymous with cash settlement
The plan is unfunded. Diversified deferred balances are liability-classified and remeasured; $5 current + $400 noncurrent = $405. Proxy settlement terms say accounts are paid through fully vested RSUs/common shares, with cash only to the extent insufficient equity-plan shares are available. Thus $405 is an economic claim, not a verified mandatory cash outflow.

Count this claim once, as a dollar deduction or equivalent shares, and reconcile with awards. The filings do not establish an exact overlap bridge from these vested deferred accounts to the October 9 RSU/PSU total. Do not subtract $405 and automatically add all possible deferred shares as well. A liability deduction is conservative only if overlapping share dilution has been excluded.

## Taxes and acquisition tax shields
Gross unrecognized tax benefits $719 plus accrued interest/penalties $113 = gross disclosed exposure $832. Only $393 of gross UTB would affect tax expense if recognized; the remainder is not proof of an independently payable cash amount or a precisely measurable DTA offset. Settlement timing/amount is expressly uncertain. No verified incremental cash liability or precise DTA netting amount is available in this work; leave those fields unresolved. Do not simply label $832 net debt, or assume gross less $393 is an exact offset.

Goodwill deductibility varies: CyberArk substantially all deductible; Koi and QRadar deductible; Chronosphere, Portkey and Protect AI not deductible. Tax-deductible goodwill does not prove book intangible amortization equals tax amortization. A blanket tax shield on all added-back book acquisition amortization is unsupported; a tax-basis/life schedule is needed.

## Share and compensation bridge

Outstanding RSUs/PSUs 29.821161m assume maximum PSU payout over full term. Options 0.232754m have $9.84 weighted strike and 9.88-year average remaining life. Treasury-stock illustration at P adds 0.232754×max(0,1−9.84/P), not gross options plus proceeds twice. Unused plan capacity 26.029643m is not an existing outstanding claim. The record-date awards table excludes ESPP.

FY26 Note15 p97 discloses $3.3bn compensation cost on unvested awards not yet recognized, amortized over approximately 2.5 years; future grants add expense. Full gross award dilution plus full retained future-service SBC can overcharge the same compensation. A selected 15m net-existing-award proxy with 0–30m sensitivity is an explicitly provisional analyst assumption, not a sourced estimate or reconciled result. A precise bridge needs earned versus future-service allocation, expected PSU performance, forfeitures, net settlement taxes and deferred-account overlap. This fact pack does not claim those missing inputs have been solved.
