## Result of the historical audit

The operating recovery is real, but its size is predominantly refining spreads. Current TTM reported EBIT is $3,800.6m; after removing inventory timing, insurance and asset gains, fire costs and all equity-method earnings, **normalized core EBIT is $3,055.9m versus a $951.4m loss**. At a constant $15 refining gross margin, the same operating-cost/volume diagnostic is **$1,444.6m versus $1,429.6m, only +1.0%**. Its cash-investment proxy declines 7.5%. This is not evidence of uninterrupted structural earnings acceleration.

Clean TTM gross refining margin is **$19.98/bbl**, versus $7.10 previously. Anchoring the revised central path at **19.98→18→16.5→15.5→15** gives **$66.16/share and 8.9% illustrative annual equity return** at $77.81. A plausible firmer path **21→19→17→16→15.5** gives **$77.61 and 12.4%**; a constructive joint case gives **$85.41 and 13.9%**. Thus the ordinary central conclusion stays watch, but a reasonable-price partial can be defensible under an explicitly firmer, sustained economics thesis. It is not forbidden by a universal discount rule and is not automatically authorized by an Opportunity score. Technical Stage 3.5 remains unverified.

## Operating normalization, without giving away recurring costs

Normalized core EBIT = reported operating EBIT + LCM/LIFO expense (negative for reversals) + fire operating expense + equity-method expense (negative for income) + formation expense + contingent-consideration expense + asset-sale expense + insurance expense (negative for insurance gains).

This removes both gains and identifiable fire response costs. It retains **SBC**, ordinary D&A, ordinary RIN/carbon cost and **RBI implementation costs**, because recurring execution expense should not vanish merely because management calls it special. Ordinary repairs and environmental upkeep remain. It excludes all SBR earnings, so SBR inventory adjustments, interest or D&A are not added back again.

## Valuation consequence and decision

The old 2027 margin of $23 is close to the latest nine-month $22.96 but above full-TTM $19.98 and much higher than 2023–25's $16.07/$7.89/$8.77. It is a firmer-cycle view, not a neutral historical fact. The revised central starts at $19.98 and fades, while keeping volume, tax, maintenance, claims, shares and the previously estimated Q4 stub unchanged. That isolates the forecast correction. Reducing the stub margin from $30 to $20 as well gives **$61.06/7.6%**, demonstrating that the central still credits near-term strength.

The original $73.08/11.0% path remains an explicit alternative. The revised ordinary central is $66.16/8.9%; a modestly firmer *persistent* margin path reaches $77.61/12.4%, near the 12.15% analytical equity hurdle, and a constructive combined margin/cost/logistics/WACC case reaches $85.41/13.9%. The latter is not a certainty or a probability-weighted expected return. A reasonable-price partial therefore has an intelligible conditional case, but the full audit does not establish an unconditional approval. An actual trend/entry/stop and market/sector check remain separate. The severe bear value is not a stop.

This appendix resolves the **historical-data omission**.
