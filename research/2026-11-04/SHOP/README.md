No source HTML or extraction utility is required. Numeric source subsets identify original SEC tables and URLs; full filings are deliberately excluded. Forecasts are analyst scenarios, not issuer guidance. Strategic investments excluded from bridge are NOT declared worthless. This is conditional research, not complete Stage3 or execution. Dates, currency, accounting changes, lending funding, EPS and uncertainty are explicit in report. Model includes full annual and terminal reinvestment fields, sensitivities and an unforced inverse scan.
