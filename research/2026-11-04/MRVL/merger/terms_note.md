# MRVL / LITE: bounded merger verification
As of 2026-11-04. Primary-source fact check and valuation implications, not legal advice or a trade recommendation.

## Announced economics and status
Definitive agreement signed November 1 and announced November 2, 2026; proposed, not completed. LITE common holders receive a fixed 4.500 MRVL shares per share. Announced ownership is approximately 67% existing MRVL / 33% LITE, fully diluted. Announcement values LITE at $115bn enterprise value, approximately $1,132/share and 23.9% premium using October 28 unaffected closing prices. These are dated reference values, not current consideration or current EV. Expected closing is H1 calendar 2028, conditional on shareholder, regulatory and customary approvals. Management identifies more than $1bn annual run-rate cost synergies and first-post-close-year non-GAAP EPS accretion; neither is realized earnings. [Announcement](https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1040/marvell-and-lumentum-to-combine-creating-a-category-leader-in-ai-connectivity)

## Material terms beyond headline
LITE Series A preferred converts on an as-converted basis at 4.500, unless holders elect one MRVL Series B preferred per LITE preferred, intended to retain equivalent rights with adjusted conversion. LITE awards convert into MRVL awards, with performance awards becoming time-based. Approvals include MRVL share issuance and charter amendment, effective S-4, Nasdaq listing and no material adverse effect. Initial outside date is November 1, 2027, conditionally extendible to February 1, May 1 and August 1, 2028 for regulatory delays. Specified recommendation-change/superior-proposal and alternative-transaction circumstances trigger MRVL $5.8bn / LITE $2.9bn fees. MRVL's $3.2bn regulatory fee applies only under specified termination conditions, with other closing conditions satisfied or capable of satisfaction. It is not an unconditional breakup payment. [Merger 8-K, Item 1.01](https://investor.marvell.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-442717/d352474d8k.htm)

## Financing and regulatory limits
Article VII does not list a financing condition; do not translate that into a claim of zero cash needs. Sections 6.15–6.16 address debt payoff documentation and convertible-note/capped-call actions. Section 6.3(d) limits required remedies: MRVL need not accept remedies materially adverse to the combined business or remedies unconditioned on consummation. Sections 7.1(c)–(d) reference disclosure-letter approval/jurisdiction schedules, so the public agreement alone does not establish the complete jurisdiction list. [Agreement, §§6.3, 6.15–6.16, 7.1–7.3](https://investor.marvell.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-442717/d352474dex21.htm)

## Analytical implications and next evidence
Fixed-stock consideration moves with MRVL: gross LITE stock consideration = 4.5 × contemporaneous MRVL price. No dollar floor is established by the headline value. Do not derive an exact share count from rounded ownership; reconcile common, preferred and awards separately. Keep standalone valuation separate from probability-weighted post-close combinations; apply integration costs, time-to-synergy and incremental dilution consistently. Latest synchronized prices, a supported standalone break value and execution costs are absent from this bounded work, so no spread, success probability or executable arbitrage return is asserted. Next evidence: S-4/proxy, shareholder vote dates, actual regulatory submissions/decisions and full Q3 results.
