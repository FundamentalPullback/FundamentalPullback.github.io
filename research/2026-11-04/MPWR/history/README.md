# MPWR financial-history normalization

As of 2026-11-04. USD absolute units (divide money by 1e6 for $m); EPS in USD/share. Fiscal year ends December 31. Interim statements unaudited. No valuation or recommendation.

## Files and reproduction
Run `python reproduce.py` using Python standard library only, offline, from any directory. `source_ledger.json` is the self-contained numeric evidence input, including original value, exact period, SEC accession and filing URL, taxonomy or rendered-label identification, filing date, retrieval date and specific caveats. Full filings are outside this package in `../raw/`; not necessary for offline reproduction. The script produces normalized long JSON/CSV, model-loading wide CSV, missing-source flags and actual validation results. It prefers the latest available filing for each exact metric/period. Original earlier figures are retained in the ledger, not silently overwritten.

Scope: FY2023–2025, Q4 2024 through Q3 2026 (latest four quarters plus preceding comparatives), 9M comparisons, TTM ended September 30 2025/2026, and balance snapshots. Direct revenue, EBIT and NI are used when available. Q4 exact NI/EBIT and cash flow quarters use labeled differences. Q4 2025 NI has a separate directly reported rounded $171.7m field, corroborating derived exact $171.656m. **Quarterly EPS is never subtracted.** Corrected Q4 2025 diluted EPS $3.49 is direct in April 30 2026 earnings commentary. Current TTM diluted EPS $18.14 is explicitly the sum of four published rounded quarterly EPS, not a company-reported TTM EPS. Corrected Q4 2024 and Q4 basic EPS were not located; missing rather than backsolved. Prior TTM EPS is therefore missing.

## Readiness
Core revenue, reported EBIT, NI, CFO, PPE, SBC and D&A schedules are loadable with provenance and explicit methods. Basic/priorTTM EPS, current intangible cash capex, all-in current FCF and some optional historical reconciliation fields remain partial. No zero-filling. `qa_flags` records missing series; absence of a missing flag does not remove the tax, control or comparability risks above. The recorded checks cover balance equations, restated FY2024 tie-out, direct corrected Q4 EPS, no quarterly EPS subtraction and SBC equality. They do not claim a complete audit of issuer accounts or forecasts.
