# TSM financial history source map

All figures are issuer primary financial statements. No USD translation is used in history. NTD thousands in full quarterly statements are divided by 1,000. Annual 20-F is already NTD millions. Cash outlays are positive inputs. `null` means unavailable/not separately disclosed, never zero.

- FY2023/2024/2025: [2025 SEC 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000162828026025362/tsm-20251231.htm), consolidated statements F6–F13. Local original `../sources/k25.html`, text `../sources/k25.txt`. Revenue, EBIT, pretax, tax and NI F6; parent NI and EPS F8; D&A/SBC/CFO F11; PPE/intangible/interest received F12; financing interest paid/lease principal F13. Parent NI distinct from consolidated NI.
- 9M2025 and comparative 9M2024: [TSMC 2025Q3 full consolidated statements](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/financial-report/2025/TSMC%202025Q3%20Consolidated%20Financial%20Statements_E_0.pdf), printed pp4–8; local `9m25.pdf/.txt`. These full statements, not rounded earnings releases, anchor Q4-25 and prior-TTM roll-forward.
- Q12026: [TSMC 2026Q1 full consolidated statements](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/financial-report/2026/TSMC%202026Q1%20Consolidated%20Financial%20Statements_E.pdf), printed pp4–8; local `q126.pdf/.txt`.
- H12026 and Q22026 income statement: [TSMC 2026Q2 full consolidated statements](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/financial-report/2026/TSMC%202026Q2%20Consolidated%20Financial%20Statements_E_2.pdf), printed pp4–8; local `../sources/h1.pdf/.txt`. Q2 cash flows are H1 minus Q1. H1 diluted EPS 49.32 vs Q1 22.08 and reported Q2 27.25 illustrates nonadditivity of rounded weighted EPS; directly reported Q2 27.25 is retained.
- Q32026 and 9M2026: [TSMC 3Q26 condensed statements](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-10/3a94d1034b9e6b22446b674f7aaf3d088a12a193/FS.pdf), PDF pp2–4; local `q326.pdf/.txt`. These are unaudited, rounded NTD millions. They do not separate intangible cash capex, SBC, lease principal, gross interest P&L or gross depreciation/amortization. Those gaps propagate to TTM instead of being filled with zero. D&A total is disclosed.

## Reproduction and interpretation
Run `python build_history.py` to regenerate `source_inputs.json` and `history.json`. The script includes transcribed raw inputs, null propagation, derivations and ratio calculations. Input values can be checked against above pages and local source text. Do not publish full filings/PDFs; public deliverable can use the two JSON files, script and this source map.

EBIT = reported income from operations; no interest/nonoperating gains added. Tax = income-tax expense, not cash taxes. CFO = issuer IFRS operating cash flow, which excludes financing interest paid and investing interest received. `cfo_after_interest_paid` deducts cash interest without adding interest income. It is a comparison aid, not automatically an unlevered owner-earnings measure. Cash interest paid may include capitalized borrowing costs; do not equate it to P&L finance costs.

`fcf_ppe_only_reported_cfo` = reported CFO less gross cash PPE acquisitions. Annual/prior-TTM PPE+intangibles FCF is separately available. Latest TTM total capex / all-in FCF is not observable in Q3 condensed source and remains null. Financing lease principal and investing cash ROU acquisitions are separate adjustments. H1 cash ROU acquisitions NTD9,441.034M and lease principal2,249.689M are disclosed but Q3 not separately available.

FY2025 diluted ADR EPS reported327.34 NTD differs from rounded ordinary65.47 ×5=327.35. Calculated ADR EPS and TTM EPS are expressly approximate. All annual-to-quarter derivations mix 0.1M annual precision with 0.001M interim precision and latest rounded-million release precision; extra decimal places are arithmetic, not economic accuracy.
