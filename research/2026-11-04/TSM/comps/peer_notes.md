# Foundry operating context, 4 November 2026

PARTIAL fundamental comparison only. No homogeneous valuation certificate, implied fair value or recommendation.

UMC Q3 revenue NT$74,239m and EBIT NT$18,643m imply reported operating margin 25.1%; GFS revenue $1,899m and EBIT $230m imply 12.1%. The parent-supplied TSM margin is 58.7%. This supports an operating-profitability gap, not automatic multiple equivalence: mature/specialty foundries have different technology, customers, growth and reinvestment needs.

UMC's current quarter recovery includes 92% utilization. Its additional board budget is not the same as FY2026 capex guidance. Q2 investment income makes net earnings an unreliable operating comparator. UMC's reported capex and the visible cash purchase lines do not fully reconcile; retain both definitions rather than silently overwriting either.

GFS's 17.9% adjusted margin must not be compared with TSM's reported margin as if equivalent. Its net loss includes a large non-operating equity issuance charge. Grants materially change its FCF presentation. November 3 prices precede November 4 earnings and therefore cannot show the market's reaction to them.

Cashflow classification remains incomplete. The condensed releases do not fully expose interest paid/received and dividend-receipt classifications. Do not call CFO-minus-capex unlevered FCF or compare cash conversion as fully standardized. UMC uses Taiwan IFRS and explicitly distinguishes it from IASB IFRS. Detailed accounting-policy and TTM evidence is still required.
