# Perimeter and comparability

## Consolidated versus shareholder economics

Revenue, operating income, consolidated net income, CFO, capital expenditures, SBC, DD&A, debt and cash use FCX's reported consolidated perimeter. The general full-consolidation rule includes 100% of PTFI and Cerro Verde despite minority holders. Morenci is the important exception: FCX recognizes its72% undivided joint-venture interest by proportionate consolidation, not100% followed by NCI. Therefore do not apply another72% haircut to reported FCX Morenci results.

PTFI ownership is48.76%, with control retained through governance. Cerro Verde ownership was53.56% before September2024,55.08% after that acquisition, and55.66% after the May2026 purchase. El Abra is51%. Net income attributable to common stockholders and diluted EPS are after the applicable NCI allocation; consolidated CFO is not. No single blanket percentage converts consolidated FCF to cash distributable to FCX shareholders.

PTFI economic attribution changed from approximately81% through2022 to48.76% from2023. This breaks simple comparisons of FCX-attributable earnings across2022/2023 even where consolidated performance is comparable. Historical tax settlements can retain the former attribution. Do not use the2019–2022 attributable margin as a steady-state modern shareholder margin.

## Cash and capital

At September2026 debt9,398 less unrestricted cash4,335 equals5,063 consolidated net debt. This differs from management's approximately1.8bn figure that excludes approximately3.2bn of PTFI downstream-facility debt. Restricted cash424 (279current and145noncurrent) is separately shown and is not deducted from debt. NCI book equity12,632 is not an NCI market valuation.

CFO minus capex is only a consolidated cash-flow diagnostic. Deducting SBC does not make it owner earnings. Minority-holder distributions, lease principal, asset-retirement payments, parent access restrictions, sustaining versus growth investment and exceptional insurance proceeds require separate analysis. The nine-month2026 release includes699 of mud-rush insurance proceeds in operating cash flow and an operating gain699 in EBIT; do not treat the resulting cash or EBIT as automatically recurring. This package retains reported values rather than silently normalizing them.

## Definitions and extraction checks

Sales uses `us-gaap:Revenues`, including provisionally priced sales effects, not merely `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`. EBIT means reported operating income, not EBITDA or pretax earnings plus interest. Net income consolidated uses `ProfitLoss`; attributable income uses reported common-stockholder income. For FCX the tag `NetIncomeLoss` alone is not safely interchangeable with consolidated net income.

For EPS, the annual diluted calculation excludes earnings allocated to participating securities. Therefore attributable net income divided by diluted shares is not always the company's direct EPS calculation. Direct annual and quarterly EPS values are retained; TTM EPS is the sum of four individually rounded quarters and is labelled accordingly.

FY2019's raw NCI tag has a sign inconsistent with the bridge between the chosen consolidated and attributable net income. The normalized NCI is derived as consolidated minus attributable; the original signed tag is retained separately in numeric inputs. No sign change is hidden. The CFO25 discrepancy is detailed in README.md and remains unresolved.
