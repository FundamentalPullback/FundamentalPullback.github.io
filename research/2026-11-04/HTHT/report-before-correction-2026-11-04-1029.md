## Business quality, durability and the pullback thesis

The durable asset is the hotel brand, member distribution and operating system, not simply China hotel property. Hanting, Ji Hotel and the wider brand ladder let franchisees fund most new hotel development while H World earns management/franchise, reservation, technology and manager-service revenue. At 2025 year end the loyalty program had over 311m members; about 77% of legacy China room nights were sold through owned channels. The company typically earns ongoing franchise fees of 3%–6.5% of hotel revenue and contracts generally last 8–10 years in China. Manager reimbursements and upfront-fee amortization are part of reported M&F revenue, so it is not a pure royalty stream. [2025 annual report, business and revenue recognition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1483994/000110465926048058/htht-20251231x20f.htm)

June 30 scale was **13,539 hotels and 1.335m rooms**, including 13,417 China hotels. Q2 China opened 498 hotels and closed 176; 497 openings were managed/franchised. This is a real shift toward lighter capital intensity, not elimination of the legacy leased estate. Q2 M&F revenue grew 25.2%, while leased/owned revenue fell 4.9%. The latest 2026 guide is 4%–8% total revenue growth, 7%–11% China and 16%–20% M&F. These are management ranges; the valuation's 2026 revenue anchor is RMB26.8bn, approximately the total midpoint. [Q2 growth, network and guidance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1483994/000110465926097496/tm2623383d1_ex99-1.htm)

The important contrary evidence: Q2 China blended RevPAR rose from RMB235 to RMB238, but **same-hotel RevPAR fell 3%, from RMB240 to RMB233**, with flat ADR and occupancy down 2.4 points. New hotel/brand mix is not the same as pricing power at established hotels. International RevPAR declined from US$102 to US$98 and occupancy from 74.0% to 70.5%. A successful franchise rollout can offset this, but franchisee economics, closures, fee collections and occupied-room growth must support the valuation. [Operating metrics](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1483994/000110465926097496/tm2623383d1_ex99-1.htm)

## What changes the decision and Stage 3.5

Evidence that upgrades the current price: stabilized same-hotel China RevPAR/occupancy while M&F growth stays strong, sustained franchisee openings without rising closures/receivables, international cash break-even and then improvement, continued buybacks/dividends without depletion of operating liquidity, and renewed guidance supporting growth above the base fade. Weak China room economics, fee concessions, slower net openings, renewed European lease losses or blocked cash remittance weaken it. Q3 date confirmation itself is an execution check, not the missing fundamental analysis.

**Stage3 is complete. Stage3.5 is not yet a verified entry.** The dated chart shows a rebound from an October28 close of45.10 to48.91; that alone is not proof of a valid support retest or the required longer-term trend. Before any entry, verify broad market and China travel/consumer conditions, actual trend/relative strength, a specific invalidation level, liquidity and event exposure. The 3:1 requirement is (credible trade target −entry)/(entry −actual technical stop), allowing for gaps and costs. The $14.60 fundamental bear value is **not** a stop, and a bull/base valuation ratio is not 3:1 trade evidence. A reasonable-price partial, if deliberately chosen under the firmer case, still needs this separate setup and portfolio risk budget.
