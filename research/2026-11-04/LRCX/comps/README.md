# LRCX / KLAC / ASML relative cross-check

Run reproduce.py offline. USD millions for Lam and KLA, EUR millions for ASML. Prices are November 4 regular closes, ASML conversion uses the ECB November 4 reference1.1267. Financial statements are US GAAP; sources and periods are retained in inputs.json.

TTM is annual plus latest quarter less prior quarter for Lam/KLA, sum of four quarters for ASML. The panel is an operating/relative cross-check, not a median target price. Gross balance-date cash and debt proxies differ from the selected Lam DCF claim bridge. ASML uses diluted weighted-average shares and nominal-debt rollforward; KLA exact lease liabilities were not separately reconciled. These limitations are visible in the output. All source data were independently derived in the research work and source URLs are supplied; no full third-party documents are distributed.
