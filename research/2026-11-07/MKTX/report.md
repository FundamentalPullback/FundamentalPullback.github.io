## Reproducible, conditional payoff math

At the scanner price, cash consideration less price = $1.41, or **0.8515% gross**. If the investor actually qualifies for the one declared dividend, the combined gross amount is $2.19, or **1.3225%**. No subsequent dividend is assumed; record date alone is not a substitute for verifying ex-dividend and settlement entitlement. Taxes, fees, funding costs and slippage are excluded.

The model's hypothetical 31 March 2027 closing produces 2.1573% mechanically annualized without dividends or 3.3626% with the one dividend. These are conditional timing illustrations, **not expected returns**, and the date is not a company commitment. Annualization uses the 6 November price date, calendar days, and (terminal proceeds/price)^(365/days)−1. Dividends are conservatively aggregated at closing for this illustration, not reinvested or separately discounted. There is no assigned completion probability, break price or target price. Delay reduces conditional annualization. Failure can create losses unrelated to the small spread.
