The script uses explicitly transcribed numeric facts and arithmetic, and regenerates inputs.json, continuation_inputs.json and qa.json. All amounts are USD millions except share prices and rates. Reported, derived, diagnostic, unverified scanner and missing model fields are labeled. Calculations use full numeric precision; report figures are rounded.
