Fundamental Pullback · SLGN · 7 November 2026

Silgan: cash quality before a transformative deal

Watchlist initiation. No current fair value or entry approval. Opportunity 80 prioritizes research; it does not resolve declining underlying volumes, inventory-accounting benefits or the new financing and minority-interest structure.

Decision and evidence

The central question is whether higher-value dispensing and genuine operating improvements can create durable cash growth after reinvestment, while Trivium financing and the 40% outside ownership leave enough value for existing shareholders. The positive case is a resilient food-packaging franchise and potentially attractive synergy execution. The adverse case is weak volumes, inflated interpretation of adjusted earnings and paying too much for a leveraged integration.

Conditional ten-year FCFF diagnostic

These are legacy-business stress calculations, not SLGN’s current fair value. They begin with TTM recurring EBITA and the September balance sheet, use generic year 1–10 annual periods, and deliberately exclude the pending transaction. They have no verified stub-period cash bridge to November 6. Results must not populate the current intrinsic-value or margin-of-safety field.

For every forecast year and terminal year: operating NOPAT = EBITA × (1 − 26%). Net growth investment = max(positive ΔNOPAT / incremental ROIC, positive Δrevenue / sales-to-capital ratio + ΔNWC). Thus margin improvement consumes capital too. ΔNWC = positive Δrevenue × normalized working-capital ratio; it is included once. Growth capex = net growth investment − ΔNWC. Total capex = replacement maintenance + growth capex. FCFF = operating NOPAT + declining amortization tax shield + depreciation − total capex − ΔNWC − the separate Central States payment. There is no terminal reinvestment holiday.

Trivium: the bridge that must be built

The announced transaction values Trivium at approximately $3.4bn EV, or 7.1x LTM adjusted EBITDA before synergies. Contributed Silgan metal/flat-cap businesses are valued at $3.8bn EV, or 8.1x. AIP contributes approximately $1bn for 40%; Silgan retains 60% and receives approximately $2.3bn cash intended to retire debt. MCC will be fully consolidated and has committed approximately $4.9bn term loans plus a $1bn revolving facility. The latter is capacity, not all drawn debt. The $3.1bn assumed Trivium obligations must be reconciled to the new financing, not blindly added again. Silgan Trivium partnership announcement; Silgan September 2026 Form 10-Q

At the announced approximate valuations, $1bn/40% suggests $2.5bn total partnership equity; $7.2bn combined contribution/acquisition EV less that equity suggests roughly $4.7bn net debt before financing fees, cash and other adjustments. This is only a reasonableness bridge, not an exact closing capitalization. It is directionally consistent with substantial new MCC financing. Treating the $2.3bn receipt as free debt relief while retaining 100% of existing earnings and all synergies would overstate value.

A defensible close-case equity calculation is: value remaining Dispensing Solutions FCFF minus its allocated net debt, plus 60% × (MCC enterprise value − MCC net debt − other senior claims), minus parent/integration costs not already modeled. Alternatively consolidate 100% and subtract the fair value of the 40% minority. Do one, not both. The carved-out flat-cap business crosses current segment boundaries, so the old three-segment statements are insufficient for a clean current target without a carve-out bridge.

Management expects $75m/$150m/$225m annual synergies in years 1/2/3 and at least 15% adjusted EPS and FCF accretion at full run rate. These are claims, not achieved free cash. Incremental capex, restructuring cash costs, stranded overhead, tax, financing costs and antitrust remedies must be financed. A no-close/delay scenario is essential. The November 6 lender consent permits guarantor and equity-pledge releases upon the entities’ unrestricted designation, subject to the stated conditions. Business contributions are restricted to reasonably prior to, or in connection with, the transaction; designation reverses if the transaction is terminated. The consent also permits specified debt-redemption use of proceeds. It is progress, not proof that all releases or regulatory closing conditions are complete. Silgan Trivium partnership announcement; Silgan lender consent Form 8-K

The diagnostic terminal EV/EBITDA range 4.9–8.4x is below or near these transaction/metal-packaging checks rather than assuming a high-growth rerating. The low bear multiple is a stress result of higher discount and reinvestment burden; it is not an observed peer quote. Public multiples use distinct EBITDA definitions and ownership/debt conventions. What proves or kills the thesis

  • Prove: dispensing demand and mix improve without relying on pass-through; metal volumes stabilize; earnings hold after removing new inventory-layer benefits.
  • Prove: fourth-quarter receivables collections and normalized inventory turn convert profit into cash without unusually extended supplier financing or outstanding checks.
  • Prove: verified carve-out FCFF, closing debt/cash/minority bridge and costed integration plan support value above price under non-bull assumptions.
  • Kill/reassess: persistent volume declines and delayed manufacturing-cost recovery, cash conversion below normalized expectations, larger integration spend, adverse approval remedies or a materially more expensive capital stack.
  • Entry remains pending: current OHLC history and a refreshed regular-session price when considering entry, EMA trends, support, confirmation method, stop, resistance, account/risk capacity and an approved current valuation.

Exact gaps versus ordinary assumptions

Missing decision-critical work: deal carve-out financials and fully allocated after-financing ownership value; verified cash/debt and seasonal bridge to the valuation date; final tax treatment, integration cost/capex and financing details; technical trend and entry inputs; the November 6 regular close is independently verified. These gaps prevent a current fair value, margin of safety or entry level. No operation was placed.

Ordinary scenario assumptions, not missing historical data: future growth, normalized restructuring allowance, margins, tax rate, capital productivity, incremental ROIC, maintenance replacement cost, NWC floor, excess-cash reserve, dilution and discount/terminal rates. Those can and should be varied; substituting assumptions for actual undisclosed transaction amounts must remain explicit.

Evidence confidence: high for directly read filing facts and accounting identity checks; medium for rounded quarter reconstruction; conditional for management outlook.
