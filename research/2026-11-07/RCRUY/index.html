Business interpretation and counterevidence

HR Technology Q1 revenue rose 20.9% in USD versus 33.2% in JPY: translation matters. US average revenue per job grew 35%; segment EBITDA+S margin reached 47.4%. Staffing's margin was 6.2%, versus 6.6% previously. Japan outlook includes gross-to-net accounting changes and deliberate withdrawal from unprofitable business. Model each business separately before consolidation; the high-margin recruitment platform cannot justify applying its economics to staffing revenue. Q1 summary.

Management attributes growth to monetization and AI-enabled hiring productivity, but explicitly notes fast increases in client spending and forecasting uncertainty. The June 2026 Japan staffing antitrust inspection has no reasonably quantified financial impact and is excluded from guidance. This is a real unresolved risk, not an assumed zero. Call transcript.

Historical restructuring reduced roughly 1,300 Indeed/Glassdoor employees in July 2025. Do not assume restructuring savings repeat indefinitely or remove recurring costs automatically. Workforce notice. HR Solutions moved from Matching & Solutions into HR Technology effective April 2025; use reconciled comparative segment data, not spliced historical segment totals. Governance change, FY2025 release.

Executed buybacks through October: 16,384,200 shares for ¥178.136bn against ¥350bn authority. Only 28,200 shares were bought in October. The period was extended to March 31, 2027. Remaining authorization is not cash already spent or free EPS growth. Refresh both cash and shares together. November repurchase update.
