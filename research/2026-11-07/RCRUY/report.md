## Business interpretation and counterevidence

HR Technology Q1 revenue rose 20.9% in USD versus 33.2% in JPY: translation matters. US average revenue per job grew 35%; segment EBITDA+S margin reached 47.4%. Staffing's margin was 6.2%, versus 6.6% previously. Japan outlook includes gross-to-net accounting changes and deliberate withdrawal from unprofitable business. Model each business separately before consolidation; the high-margin recruitment platform cannot justify applying its economics to staffing revenue. [Q1 summary](https://recruit-holdings.com/en/ir/library/upload/Recruit_202703Q1_summary_en.html).

Management attributes growth to monetization and AI-enabled hiring productivity, but explicitly notes fast increases in client spending and forecasting uncertainty. The June 2026 Japan staffing antitrust inspection has no reasonably quantified financial impact and is excluded from guidance. This is a real unresolved risk, not an assumed zero. [Call transcript](https://recruit-holdings.com/en/ir/library/upload/Recruit_202703Q1_call-transcript_en.html).

Historical restructuring reduced roughly 1,300 Indeed/Glassdoor employees in July 2025. Do not assume restructuring savings repeat indefinitely or remove recurring costs automatically. [Workforce notice](https://recruit-holdings.com/en/newsroom/20250711_0001/). HR Solutions moved from Matching & Solutions into HR Technology effective April 2025; use reconciled comparative segment data, not spliced historical segment totals. [Governance change](https://recruit-holdings.com/en/newsroom/20240909_0001/), [FY2025 release](https://recruit-holdings.com/en/ir/library/upload/Recruit_202603Q4_earnings_en.html).

Executed buybacks through October: 16,384,200 shares for ¥178.136bn against ¥350bn authority. Only 28,200 shares were bought in October. The period was extended to March 31, 2027. Remaining authorization is not cash already spent or free EPS growth. Refresh both cash and shares together. [November repurchase update](https://recruit-holdings.com/en/newsroom/20261102_0001/).

## Exact post-event work
1. Retrieve November 11 IFRS results, segment schedules, guidance and call. Confirm publication date and fiscal labels; Q2 means July–September 2026, not calendar Q2.
2. Complete FY2023–FY2025 plus Q3/Q4 FY2025 and Q1/Q2 FY2026; derive discrete quarters from cumulative statements. Build current and prior TTM on identical definitions. Reconcile April 2025 perimeter and gross-to-net effects.
3. Separate job volumes, ARPJ, employer spend/retention, premium product mix, FX and acquisitions. Test whether monetization remains durable without worsening customer economics.
4. Reconcile EBITDA+S to EBIT, retaining SBC and necessary costs. Separate ROU depreciation, software investment, restructuring cash, taxes and working capital. Investigate why management gross cash differs from IFRS cash equivalents rather than assuming all financial assets are surplus.
5. Refresh debt, leases, pensions, litigation/provisions and legally available excess cash; reconcile treasury, trusts, options and executed repurchases. Avoid double-counting lease cost/debt and SBC/dilution.
6. If evidence supports valuation, construct ten-year operating FCFF with five detailed years and five transition years; use currency-consistent discount inputs and segment-specific economics. Tie terminal reinvestment to NOPAT × g/ROIC, with WACC > g; disclose terminal weight. Run adverse/base/favorable, sensitivities and alternative inverse solutions through the same model. No free future buybacks or acquisition growth.
7. Cross-check with BZ/SEEK for platform economics and Randstad/Adecco/Robert Half for staffing, using verified same-date financial definitions. China exposure, accounting, geography and business mix prevent mechanical multiple averaging.
8. Resolve ADR volume conflict and 20-session average, trading restrictions, spread, ratio/corporate actions, matched-time FX and trend evidence. Approval remains pending until all essential information is reconciled.

## Pros and cons
Pros: accelerated platform monetization, strong reported quarterly cash generation, limited reported borrowings and demonstrated capital returns. Cons: demanding expectations, currency effects, staffing margin pressure, unresolved antitrust exposure, SBC/reinvestment obligations, unsponsored OTC mechanics and inconsistent volume evidence. These do not net to an approval score.
