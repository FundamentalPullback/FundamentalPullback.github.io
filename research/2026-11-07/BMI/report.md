## Business and recovery evidence

BMI sells water measurement, communications, software and monitoring solutions to utilities and other water-oriented customers. Installed hardware plus connected software supports recurring relationships, but installation schedules and short-cycle municipal orders make hardware revenue uneven. This is not a pure subscription company.

Q1 sales and EBIT fell sharply; Q2 remained below prior year; Q3 restored modest reported sales and operating-profit growth. Management attributes the slowdown to project pacing and weak short-cycle demand, with awarded projects starting deployment, rather than lost competitiveness [S5,S4,S3]. That explanation is credible enough to monitor, not sufficient to assume every postponed dollar returns. Organic utility revenue was still down in Q3. Software mix and cost control helped margins, including lower incentives: margin improvement alone does not prove volume recovery.

Q3 sales were $235.938m (+0.1% YoY), EBIT $47.742m (+3.6%), net income $35.903m (+2.4%) and EPS $1.24 (+4.2%). EPS benefited from fewer diluted shares as well as operations. Q3 utility revenue excluding UDlive was down 3% YoY but up 5% sequentially. Latest management commentary expects Q4 base sales to grow sequentially and YoY, continuing into 2027 [S3]. Earlier full-year flattish guidance is dated context, not a numeric Q3 reaffirmation.

The year-to-date decline and valuation reset are plausible contributors to the drawdown. This is an inference, not an event study attributing every price move. A recovered quote does not invalidate operating weakness. Confirm order conversion, normalized incentives, electronics costs, and recurring software growth before elevating the thesis.

## Ten-year FCFF: explicit funding, not a target-fitting model

Valuation time zero is the 6 November 2026 regular close. Year 1 is 7 November 2026–6 November 2027, and year 10 ends 6 November 2036; years 6–10 fade growth to terminal, with terminal year ending 6 November 2037. End-year discounting is exactly t=1 through t=10. The last September 30 TTM is used as the run-rate base without a separately modeled 37-day October 1–November 6 stub. This is a provisional run-rate convention, not FY2027, fiscal guidance, or an assertion that those 37 days had zero cash flow. A fiscal-calendar/stub reconciliation is required before an actionable valuation. Common tax rate 25%. All future figures are analyst assumptions, not consensus.

FCFF = NOPAT + D&A − total cash investment − Δoperating working capital. To avoid unfunded margin expansion, net reinvestment is max(0, annual NOPAT increase)/incremental ROIC. D&A is 4.3% of sales, close to the primary TTM $37.901m / $881.242m. ΔNWC is 15% of incremental sales. Total cash investment = D&A + net reinvestment − ΔNWC. It includes maintenance, internal software and growth spending; it is not a prediction of the PPE cash-flow line. Gross investment is explicitly floored at 100% of modeled D&A after funding ΔNWC, in every projected year and the terminal year. This floor adds no cash outflow in the original three cases, so central assumptions and values are unchanged. The replacement allowance is an analyst proxy covering physical assets and software, not a verified maintenance budget. This charges investment even for margin-led NOPAT growth, a conservative analytical convention, not a measured project return. No capital release is credited in contraction.

This framework funds the modeled growth and replaces amortizing assets economically, but needs a business-level investment build before adoption. It may be conservative where purchased-intangible amortization exceeds actual replacement needs. Do not claim historical capex supports every forecast dollar or that the resulting DCF is uniquely correct.

Terminal FCFF = year-11 NOPAT ×(1−g/terminal ROIC). Terminal ROIC exceeds WACC but fades below growth-period assumptions; persistence depends on installed relationships/software mix. The higher-return favorable case requires more execution than currently demonstrated.

| Adverse | 0%,3%,4%,4%,4% | 18%→18.5% | 10.5% | 2.0% | 15%→15% |

Central growth approximates management's high-single-digit long-run framework, followed by maturation, rather than treating negative 2026 growth as permanent. Adverse assumes weak project conversion; favorable requires sustained share/mix execution. Central margin recovery is above FY2025 reported 20%, with every increment funded. A 24% favorable margin is an assumption beyond currently demonstrated full-year GAAP margins.

WACC is analyst-selected for a nearly unlevered USD business, not copied from a data vendor. Context: early-November risk-free yields are around 4.8–4.9% [S16; exact dated header unavailable in the readable extract]. Central 9% corresponds approximately to 4.85% risk free + 0.9 business beta ×4.6% assumed ERP. Beta and ERP here are assumptions, not independently fitted observations. 8.5–10.5% scenarios test execution/macro risk. The vendor's lower historical beta does not automatically justify a 7% discount rate during project uncertainty.

### Replacement-capital and inverse QA

All four inverse solves scan 1,000 intervals across 0–50% annual revenue growth, verify nondecreasing prices, identify exactly one bracket, and enforce absolute price residual below $0.0000001. Endpoint prices, original/final brackets and residuals are stored with each inverse in model_outputs.json. This verifies numerical behavior on the scanned domain, not global uniqueness outside it.

The gross investment floor is applied after working-capital funding: investment=max(D&A+growth reinvestment−ΔNWC, D&A×floor multiplier). It operates in all ten years and the terminal year. Thus the aggregate g/ROIC model cannot finance working capital by silently cutting the assumed replacement allowance. The 100% floor never changes the three original values. At a 150% replacement floor, adverse/central/favorable become $43.54/$78.51/$119.96; at 200%, $36.19/$69.01/$107.23. These are stress diagnostics, not newly supported maintenance estimates. Physical/software spending still lacks a verified separate build. Each case/floor test records minimum funding headroom and additional explicit/terminal outflow.
