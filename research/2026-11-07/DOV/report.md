## Reproducible ten-year FCFF and expectations
The supplied script computes all historical bridges, forecasts, sensitivity and inverse valuation. Valuation timing uses ten full forward years from the November 6 price comparison, with year1 cash discounted for one full year. There is no November–December stub or midyear convention. The FY2026 guidance baseline is a scale anchor rather than a claim that the first forecast period is calendar2026. It is a deterministic research model, not a banker-formula workbook. Baseline revenue8699.514 is FY2025 multiplied by the 7.5% company-guidance midpoint, not reported FY2026.

Tax23%, NWC20% of incremental sales, depreciation2.1%; every case retains SBC and amortization. Explicit reinvestment is MAX(physical net investment + incremental NWC, positive incremental NOPAT / incremental ROIC). This funds margin improvement as well as growth rather than granting free productivity capital. Years6–10 growth fades to terminal; margins remain at year5. Terminal reinvestment is MAX(NOPAT×g/ROIC, continuing physical net investment + incremental NWC). Thus the terminal does not erase the physical investment burden. Assumed ROIC is not measured historical marginal ROIC.

Central WACC is calibrated, not measured: latest available November5 ten-year Treasury4.84% in the November6 Fed release; ERP5%, beta1.15 and USD borrowing cost6% are assumptions. With roughly89.3% market equity weight and tax23%, indicated WACC rounds to10%. The euro coupon is not a USD discount rate. Use9–11% sensitivity instead of selecting a favorable rate to approve. [Rates](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/)

Terminal values contribute 46.3% / 55.9% / 65.7% of scenario EV. Central WACC9–11% and terminal growth2–3% imply $76.92–114.63; this is parameter sensitivity, not a confidence interval. A 1 percentage-point increase in an ongoing economic amortization add-back would require a separately justified replacement reserve before being treated as distributable cash.

At central margins and reinvestment, the inverse model needs roughly24–26% annual sales growth for five years before fading to2.5%, versus current organic guidance5–6%. This is a mathematical diagnostic, not consensus or a plausible forecast. It partly reflects deliberately conservative amortization and reinvestment policies. Removing all economic amortization would overstate value; changing the economic-replacement assumption requires evidence. The model does not claim the market must be forecasting this exact growth path.

The margins are **not comparable accounting measures** and are not averaged. Neither benchmark justifies giving Dover ITW profitability. ## What changes the conclusion
Reopen after financing/tender settlement reconciliation and the November10 presentation; verify backlog conversion, organic growth, food-retail execution, cash tied in receivables/inventory and recurring restructuring. Upside requires better sustainable margins or lower economic replacement needs than this model, with supporting evidence. Downside is order normalization plus working-capital lockup, capex still required, and a higher discount rate. Acquisition failures or tax/legal leakage reduce equity value.

Exact missing work: November cash/debt/FX/fees reconciliation; current existing-award schedule; current pension/finance lease balances; retained-perimeter full-cycle series and historical constant-perimeter growth; capex/software decomposition; contemporaneous quantitative peer valuation. Ordinary assumptions: forecast growth/margins,23% tax,300 operating cash,0.8m award proxy, capital intensity, NWC, ROIC and discount-rate parameters. These categories must not be confused. Scanner Stage2/EMA/volume and execution controls are absent. No price ceiling, stop, quantity, order or purchase approval is produced.
