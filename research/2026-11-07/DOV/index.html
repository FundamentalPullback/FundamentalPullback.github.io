FY2024 continuing profit includes $597.8m disposition gains; net income also contains ESG discontinued-operation gain. Neither supports recurring EPS or FCFF. De-Sta-Co was sold in March 2024 without discontinued classification, so raw FY2023/24 revenue is not a perfectly constant perimeter. FY2025 acquisitions cost about $665m; Q3 2026 includes Cloeren ($39.25m). Do not attribute purchased growth to organic demand. Pending Leistung consideration is approximately $40m; the prospective equity-method sale proceeds are approximately $43m. Neither is booked as completed in this model. Annual notes3–4; Q3 notes3/20.

SBC remains an expense. Acquired amortization also remains in model EBIT and is not added back; only physical depreciation is added back. This is conservative economic-replacement treatment, not proof that GAAP useful lives equal economic decay. FY2025 depreciation was $169.25m versus amortization $210.33m; 9M2026 amortization $160.16m. Forecast physical depreciation 2.1% of sales and capex 3.0–3.2% are assumptions grounded in, but above, recent investment rates. Software is not separately identified; no extra software add-back is granted. R&D stays expensed. A detailed maintenance/growth/software investment decomposition is an outstanding validation item, not invented actual data.

Restructuring recurs ($66.6m charges in 9M2026 versus $32.4m prior year); the model does not automatically remove it. Tax is normalized to 23%, rather than extrapolating discrete tax benefits. Cash growth includes tax timing and working-capital effects: 9M receivables and inventory consumed $395.3m versus $129.4m prior year. FCF improvement therefore does not prove permanently higher conversion. Q4 is seasonally cash-heavy; do not annualize Q1. Q3 cash statement/restructuring section; Annual accounting notes.

What changes the conclusion

Reopen after financing/tender settlement reconciliation and the November10 presentation; verify backlog conversion, organic growth, food-retail execution, cash tied in receivables/inventory and recurring restructuring. Upside requires better sustainable margins or lower economic replacement needs than this model, with supporting evidence. Downside is order normalization plus working-capital lockup, capex still required, and a higher discount rate. Acquisition failures or tax/legal leakage reduce equity value.

Exact missing work: November cash/debt/FX/fees reconciliation; current existing-award schedule; current pension/finance lease balances; retained-perimeter full-cycle series and historical constant-perimeter growth; capex/software decomposition; contemporaneous quantitative peer valuation. Ordinary assumptions: forecast growth/margins,23% tax,300 operating cash,0.8m award proxy, capital intensity, NWC, ROIC and discount-rate parameters. These categories must not be confused.
