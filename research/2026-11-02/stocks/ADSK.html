Business thesis, evidence and failure tests

Autodesk’s design/build/manufacture installed base, interoperability and professional workflows support recurring revenue and switching costs. Q2 FY27 recurring revenue was 97% of sales; constant-currency net revenue retention was near the top of the 100–110% band. Revenue growth alone overstates organic progress because moving channel incentives from contra-revenue to operating costs mechanically raises reported revenue without similarly raising operating profit or free cash flow. Thus the model does not simply extrapolate reported 16–18% growth.

Evidence supporting durability: Q2 revenue +16% reported/+14% constant currency, current RPO +12%, and broad product growth. Evidence demanding restraint: total RPO only +2% and unbilled commitments -8%, partly explained by shorter commitments/reduced multi-year discounts; not proof of collapse but not an unlimited backlog argument. AECO is exposed to construction cycles; AI can increase workflow value but also change seat/pricing economics and compute costs. Sales reorganization execution and retention matter more than the AI narrative.

MaintainX closed August 3 for approximately $3.53 billion net of acquired cash. It broadens operations software, but the latest quarter ends July 31 and contains no acquired operating results. Guidance includes MaintainX; a separate acquired revenue/profit run-rate and purchase-price allocation were not available in the latest 10-Q. The forecast includes the acquisition within company FY27 guidance, charges its purchase in the equity bridge, and charges ongoing replacement investment separately. It does not add a speculative MaintainX valuation on top of the consolidated DCF.

Disconfirm the thesis if current RPO and retention persistently weaken, sales restructuring damages renewals, AI pricing fails to offset costs, or acquisition growth fails to cover financing/integration costs. A stronger case requires durable organic growth after transaction-model effects fade and cash margins improving after paying SBC, not simply higher adjusted EPS. Nov24 results are the next scheduled fundamental checkpoint, not this week’s event.

Reported-to-normalized cash bridge

Company FCF 2,839 less economic SBC 706 and purchased intangibles 35 gives 2,098, not a clean FCFF yet. cash_bridge.json reconciles exactly to 1,733 normalized historical FCFF using: - Net income converted to EBIT at normalized 23% tax, removing investment/financing earnings and tax-rate distortion. - Removal of temporary deferred-tax/noncash adjustments, and replacement of working-capital/commission timing with a modest recurring customer-funding assumption. - Restructuring normalized to a recurring 0.3% revenue charge, rather than assuming all reorganization costs disappear forever. - 1.5% revenue acquisition/replacement reserve, alongside purchased-intangible investment, and no perpetual benefit from the current acquired-amortization tax shield.

This is a transparent analyst normalization, not a management cash-flow measure. The 1,733 estimate is materially lower than both reported FCF 2,839 and FCF less SBC 2,133. Contract-cost amortization is not free cash: contract assets and related payables are explicitly considered rather than adding back amortization without funding new commissions. MaintainX is outside historical TTM.

Balance, liquidity and common ownership

July31 cash and securities 4,357; existing notes principal 2,500 and commercial paper 1,000. August3 term loan adds 1,000 to cash and debt; acquisition consumes 3,530. September refinancing replaces, rather than adds to, that term borrowing; estimated net proceeds 991 versus 1,000 principal. Therefore pro-forma liquidity 1,818, debt principal 4,500 and net debt 2,682. A separately labeled 500 operating-liquidity reserve yields 3,182 deducted from operating enterprise value.

The September prospectus states intended term-loan repayment, not an independent repayment receipt. Net debt is substantially unchanged whether proceeds temporarily remain cash or repay the loan; gross maturity presentation depends on completion. New notes carry 5.05%/5.65% coupons. Revolver availability of 2,000 supports liquidity, not surplus equity value. Cash generation supports servicing, but the acquisition removes the illusion of a net-cash balance. No post-July operating cash generation or repurchases are invented in the bridge.

211 million shares is a current diluted proxy (Q2 weighted diluted), versus 209 million outstanding August21. No free future share-count reductions. 5.413 million unvested RSUs are disclosed; using all of them on top of 209 million would yield 214.413 million and lower central value to 163.81. That is a conservative overhang sensitivity, not an assertion that every unvested award is incremental to a fully economic SBC cash charge. Operating leases remain in operating expenses; no duplicate lease-debt deduction. Strategic private investments receive zero incremental value in the base; deferred-tax assets receive no separate value while tax assumptions already address operating taxes.

FCFF construction and forecast rationale

model.py is the executable source of truth, calculations.json its outputs. Ten forecast fiscal years FY28–FY37 plus an explicit 90/365-year FY27 stub from Nov2 to Jan31. FY27 revenue anchor 8,320 is company guidance midpoint, not a completed actual year. Stub uses normalized annual FCFF 1,822.7 pro-rated, discounted at 0.2466 years; FY28–FY37 at 1.2466–10.2466 years. It does not substitute seasonal reported Q4 cash for sustainable annual earnings.

Five detailed years start from the latest observed product mix, scaled to FY27 guidance: AECO growth 13/12/11/10/9%; AutoCAD 8/8/7/6/5%; Manufacturing 11/11/10/9/8%; Media 6/6/5/5/4%; Other flat. These are analyst estimates below headline reported growth, reflecting cRPO/retention and eventual exhaustion of accounting gross-up. Product mix is a disclosed proxy because separate MaintainX run-rate is not published.

Normalized cash-EBIT margins 30.5/31.5/32.5/33/33% retain SBC. The FY27 calibration is 39% non-GAAP margin minus approximately 9% SBC minus 0.3% recurring restructuring reserve =29.7%. Forward margins imply pre-SBC normalized margins 39.5/40.25/41/41.25/41%, with SBC 9/8.75/8.5/8.25/8% retained in the final net margin. This is an operating-leverage assumption, not a reported GAAP margin or an independent SBC growth engine. Acquired-intangible amortization is excluded from cash EBIT, therefore ONLY 0.6% physical/software depreciation is added back. No double amortization addback.

Fixed capex 0.9% sales approximates the company FY27 70 million guidance; purchased intangibles 0.5% tracks historical cash investment; acquisition/replacement reserve 1.5% is a conservative judgment relative to lumpy acquisition history, not a forecast of a specific deal. Working-capital funding equals -10% of incremental revenue, reflecting advance-funded subscriptions, much smaller than assuming all deferred revenue growth is cash profit. All modeled net reinvestment is positive despite that customer funding. No acquisition-driven growth is granted without the existing purchase price and continuing replenishment costs.

Years6–10 transition growth and reinvestment toward terminal 3% growth and 25% incremental ROIC; terminal reinvestment is 12% of NOPAT. High incremental ROIC is plausible for software with expensed R&D and negative operating working capital, but is not a claim that MaintainX’s purchase earned 25% immediately.
