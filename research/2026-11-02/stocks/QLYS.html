Business thesis and operating evidence

Annual subscription vulnerability/exposure-management platform, 10,000+ customers, with costly-to-replicate integrated agent/data infrastructure and recurring workflows. AI-driven exploit proliferation creates demand for detection, prioritization and remediation; it also creates new competition and budget displacement. Strong margins alone do not establish accelerating demand.

Q2 revenue182.18m (+11%), GAAP EBIT61.90m (+20%) and gross margin83%. August guidance: FY2026 revenue732–738m (+9–10%); GAAP EPS5.76–5.90, non-GAAP7.74–7.88; Q3 revenue185.5–187.5m (+9–10%) [S7]. Guidance is as of August4, not a November reaffirmation.

Federal authorization, cross-sell and QFlex are plausible catalysts, not quantified guaranteed orders. Risks: larger integrated competitors, channel concentration (53% H1 sales), upsell stagnation, autonomous-remediation errors, cyber incident/reputation, 45% international Q2 revenue/FX, and execution after leadership changes. No financial-distress evidence found.

Same-model reverse and margin of safety

At207.60, keeping central margins/rates/reinvestment requires adding 13.71 percentage points to each first-five growth assumption:23.71/23.71/22.71/21.71/20.71%, followed by the same five-year transition. Alternatively, keeping central growth requires margin shift+36.63pp, ending at 71.63% EBIT margin, economically implausible relative to83% current gross margin. At scanner202.90 growth shift still13.23pp. These are alternate solutions to one model, not independent confirmation.

This does not assert the market literally forecasts one path. Lower discount rates, higher sustainable margins and stronger growth can combine. What matters: existing105% NDER and9–10% revenue guide do not support the required reacceleration. Central value implies47.3% downside; favorable16.4% downside.
