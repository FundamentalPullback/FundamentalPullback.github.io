BZ — wait; no entry approval at $17.06

As of 2 November 2026. First FCFF research model, not replacement of an approved valuation. Historical website only contained below-threshold triage. Status: screen-grade; no Stage 3.5 approval. This is a reproducible research valuation, not an exhaustive completed Stage 3 certification.

Decision

The business improvement is real, but the low displayed P/E is misleading. Central value $17.01 is approximately the $17.06 regular close independently checked by the lead researcher. Central sensitivity $15.7–18.8; adverse/base/favorable $11.33/$17.01/$24.57, not a probability interval. There is essentially no margin of safety. A $14–14.5 research-revisit zone (roughly 15–18% below central) compensates for estimation uncertainty in capital requirements/margins, not a second arbitrary charge for China risk already reflected in WACC. It is not an authorized maximum or buy order, and unresolved bridge controls must be closed even there.

Business, expectations and first rejection

BOSS Zhipin is a Chinese online recruitment platform, not excluded Healthcare/Real Estate. Paid employers and user engagement support monetization. Q2 revenue +14.1% and EBIT +32.6% are credible operating progress; GAAP profit +173% is not a sustainable growth rate because an investment revaluation dominates the increase. The first rejection test is whether hiring demand and paying-employer growth slow while AI/server and marketing spending remain high. No verified single cause for the full 24.7% pullback was established; do not invent one. [S4]

Normalized cash and earnings

H1 2026 net RMB3,068.1m includes Zhipu revaluation gain RMB2,080.2m, tax RMB520.1m; removing the after-tax gain gives RMB1,508.0m before other normalization. The model uses EBIT, so neither investment gains nor interest on separately added financial assets enter FCFF. It does not add SBC to modeled cash. A 25% normalized operating tax rate exceeds the recent ordinary effective burden and allows for mature tax/top-up/remittance leakage; sensitivity is preferable to treating current concessions as perpetual. [S2/S4]

H1 CFO RMB2,134.9m less cash capex118.7m = FCF2,016.2m, essentially flat vs2,013.9m. Capex payable increased197.997m; economic asset additions proxied by cash capex plus that increase are316.702m. CFO minus economic investment minus SBC is1,440.7m vs about1,546.6m prior H1 (using prior capex payable change−14.186m). This diagnostic falls~6.8%, rather than showing unqualified cash acceleration. It is not formal FCFF: interest and working capital still differ. The model budgets capex6.5% of sales against D&A5.5%, not current tiny cash capex. The initial forecast capex is close to recent H1 economic investment annualized, and recognizes earlier 2023/24 investment cycles. [S1/S2/S3]

Deferred revenue supports cash generation; the model assumes zero incremental net working-capital release centrally, rather than harvesting it forever. Q2 CFO decline reflects marketing, tax and investment-income receipts; no annualization of a single quarter. Operating leases stay in expenses, and neither ROU depreciation nor lease debt is added/subtracted again.

Balance/ownership bridge

June snapshot: no interest-bearing borrowings, no pledged assets. Financial assets are not all insured cash: substantial wealth-management products and fixed notes carry counterparty/liquidity risk. June cash-like short assets exclude the separately valued listed stake. Long fixed notes and WMP added once. Zhipu stake1,486,720shares (~0.3%) marked RMB2,137.4m at June; relevant deferred tax deducted. Its November mark/lock-up realizability is not refreshed, so the bridge is research-grade. Removing the entire after-tax listed stake lowers value by only about$0.52 and does not create a buy. Unlisted investments are excluded. [S2]

The RMB3,000m analyst reserve covers working liquidity and the RMB804.9m restricted net assets disclosed at FY2025; the latter are not subtracted again as if all were separately restricted cash. Reinvestment avoids permanent D&A excess. Terminal25% ROIC is an asset-light platform judgment, not an independently observed incremental ROIC; this is a key sensitivity. Central growth starts near recent paid-customer and revenue growth, then decelerates; margin36% matches latest Q2 rather than assuming repeated huge expansion. Favorable must not be used to justify today’s price.

Same-model reverse at17.06 requires only +0.16 percentage point margin over central across forecast years (final~36.16%), or +0.09 percentage point annual growth in first five years, holding other assumptions fixed. Price is approximately central expectations, not bargain pricing. These are alternative solutions of the same model, not independent confirmations.

Comparables and invalidation

Recruit Holdings is the closest large listed recruitment-platform reference but includes staffing and matching businesses and different countries. At Nov2 JPY17,970 and company FY2026 basic EPS guidance543, headline forward P/E~33.1x. On a more homogeneous Q2 run-rate basis retaining SBC, excluding net investment income and taxing operating profit at25%, Recruit P/NOPAT~33x; BZ~20.6x (spot diluted ADS denominator), including both companies’ entire market value rather than stripping assets. This shows BZ’s discount but is not a reason to grant a Japanese/global peer multiple to a China/VIE issuer. Recruit’s faster recent growth and geography/business mix matter; SBC-excluding EBITDA+S must not be compared directly with BZ GAAP EBIT. No mechanical averaging of peer multiples and DCF. [S11/S12]

Revisit after Q3 publication (date not confirmed in consulted issuer event list), material hiring-demand change, capex/SBC surprise, guidance revision, investment realization or stock below review zone. Kill thesis on sustained paid-employer/revenue deterioration with margin compression, severe data/cybersecurity restriction, inability to transfer economic benefits, or loss of investment liquidity. The present review does not establish all required current share/cash and quarterly-capex details; confidence medium-low, not decision-grade. No position sizing or stop invented. Lead researcher owns current technical eligibility.

Reproducibility and remaining limits

Raw annual/interim PDFs and extracted text are retained. This is a controlled support calculation, not a banker formula workbook and not independently audited. Existing-award valuation uses treasury-stock method, not option-pricing fair value; minority book proxy and June investment marks are explicit limits.
