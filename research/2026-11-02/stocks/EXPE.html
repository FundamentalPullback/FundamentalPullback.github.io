WATCH / WAIT; not approved for Stage 3.5. Regular close $298.99 (parent-verified StockAnalysis history), versus scanner Last $299.49. What changed / investment case

Expedia is a consumer and B2B travel distribution platform. B2B expansion and better cost absorption are the credible thesis, not the scanner score. Q2 bookings +12%, revenue +14%, and B2B revenue +23%; Q3 company revenue guidance $4.65–4.75bn and FY2026 $16.05–16.22bn. Guidance is management's estimate, not ours. Q2 release

A single verified cause for the entire 12.6% pullback was not established. Competition from suppliers/direct channels, Google and AI distribution is a risk hypothesis, not proof of a particular session's cause. The decisive next catalyst is November 4 after regular close, confirmed by issuer calendar. Waiting for that report here is an investment judgment; there is no newly invented universal earnings blackout rule.
