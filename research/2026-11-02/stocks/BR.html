What would change the decision

Reopen after Nov3 results: organic recurring growth versus acquisitions, closed-sales conversion, actual GAAP margin/SBC, cash-tax/deferred-cost conversion, updated balance and crypto/contingency disclosures. A large central-value improvement needs evidence, not merely a price rally. Deteriorating recurring demand, sustained margin compression, expensive acquisition dependence or material accounting/control issues weaken the thesis. There is no verified accounting failure here; audit attention to digital assets is not itself an adverse audit opinion.

QA addendum — reinvestment and confidence

The D&A glide is not the issuer’s total-D&A forecast. Its direction is supported by Note11’s existing-intangible amortization runoff (2027–31:240.1/209.4/126.0/116.8/104.0m); our forecast also includes depreciation and amortization from continuing investment/acquisitions. The precise4.5→3.3% glide remains analyst judgment and needs an asset-vintage roll-forward before a purchase-grade valuation. For transparency, central net reinvestment is −14.3m in year1, then+7.7/+32.4/+58.6/+86.7m in years2–5. This small initial release comes from prior acquired-intangible amortization exceeding modeled new capital; it is not negative cash capex, and it does not fund acquisitions for free. Acquisition cost1.5% is explicitly deducted while organic growth and margin improvement carry most of the thesis.

Reducing D&A by1 percentage point of sales in each detailed year (or increasing capex1 point) lowers central value to121.33. Increasing acquisition reserve from1.5% to2.5% also gives121.33; removing it entirely raises value to131.02. These are isolated sensitivities, not adjustments to be stacked. Even adding excluded assets at full June carrying values does not bridge184.22. Therefore the wait conclusion survives reasonable reinvestment disputes, but125 must not be labeled certified fair value and106 must not be published as an approved buy price.
