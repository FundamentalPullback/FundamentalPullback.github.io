# WFC valuation method decision

Method selected for the original November 11 research under Plan v2.1 Stage 3 sections 9b and 13. The numerical scenarios remain illustrative.

Wells Fargo is a deposit-taking regulated bank. Interest expense, deposits and most funding are operating inputs; ordinary industrial FCFF, net-debt subtraction and CFO minus capital expenditure do not measure distributable equity cash. Use a ten-year residual-income model on common book equity, corroborated by the mathematically equivalent discounted potential common payout model. Earnings are after preferred dividends. Discount at an explicit cost-of-equity hurdle, not WACC; do not subtract deposits/debt or add cash again. Begin with reported common book value $52.91 at September 30 2026, not tangible book: forecasts therefore use common-equity returns, not ROTCE. No goodwill is added separately.

Status is illustrative / preliminary, not an approved value range. All forecasts and discount rates are own assumptions, not guidance or consensus. Model value depends on through-cycle common ROE, capital retention, future OCI and achievable distributions. Capital-distribution stress reconciliation and complete normalized comparable-period earnings bridge remain open. Historical bank ROE uses average equity; forecast return is on beginning equity and is labeled accordingly.

Model rules: earnings_t = beginning common book_t * forecast beginning-equity return_t; retained equity = beginning book * assumed book growth; potential common distribution = earnings - retention. End book = beginning book + retention. Future OCI = 0 in base mechanics; no assumed recovery of existing AOCI. Constant share units, with all potential returns represented as dividend-equivalent cash; no gratuitous EPS growth from buybacks. SBC remains in earnings. Terminal return and growth are stable; terminal payout = end year10 book * (terminal return - g), discounted at ke-g. Alternate residual-income expression must tie exactly to payout valuation. Severe losses beyond model scenarios are possible.

No order or Stage 3.5 authorization. No approved valuation or entry.
