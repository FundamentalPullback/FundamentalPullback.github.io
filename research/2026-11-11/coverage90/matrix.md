## Taxonomy
- **strong**: New evidence establishes a persuasive robust valuation case; no row qualified in this sweep.
- **recovery_watch**: Business or valuation improvement merits defined followup; no new buy approval or robust margin is asserted.
- **no_cushion**: Existing model/report does not establish sufficient valuation cushion; model maturity varies and is reported separately.
- **blocked**: Current total valuation or material capital/source reconciliation is unresolved; this does not mean no prior research exists.
- **event**: A scheduled catalyst or corporate transaction changes the next analytical step; it is not entry approval.
- **excluded**: Healthcare sector is outside the standing ordinary research universe.
- **uncovered**: No prior company-specific work located for an otherwise eligible symbol; zero such rows here.
