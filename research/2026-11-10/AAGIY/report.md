# AIA: cash distributions require more than surplus generation

10 November 2026. AAGIY represents four AIA ordinary shares per ADR. The Nov 9 scanner price was $40.35, with Opportunity 80 and Quality 80. This update continues A 375 and does not grant an entry approval.

AIA targets distributions of 75% of annual net free-surplus generation, not 75% of its accumulated surplus. For the twelve months through June 2026, reported net generation was $4.779 bn, while actual subsidiary capital flows were $3.591 bn. Observed holding interest and corporate uses totaled $687 m. Timing differences matter: this comparison does not itself predict a dividend reduction.

H 1 shareholder returns of $3.649 bn exceeded both net generation of $2.758 bn and subsidiary flows of $1.708 bn. Holding financial resources fell from $10.507 bn to $8.235 bn. That resource measure includes securities, funds and on-demand subsidiary notes, net of repos; cash alone was $542 m. It is not excess cash available to shareholders, and holding borrowings were $14.320 bn.

The latest full financial information remains the June interim report. October 30 was a new-business update: Q 3 value of new business was $1.415 bn, down 5% at constant currency, and nine-month value was $4.627 bn, up 5%. No quarterly IFRS earnings or cash-flow figures are inferred from that release. The official calendar does not yet supply a next exact earnings date.

An insurer distribution framework must separately test subsidiary remittance permissions, holding liquidity, capital buffers, debt refinancing and reserve risk. The June shareholder-capital ratio of 210% and group LCSM ratio of 229% are different measures; neither proves all surplus is transferable. Actuarial embedded value, financial resources and free surplus are overlapping views, not additive equity assets.

A conditional distribution model is being assessed using explicit remittance and reserve assumptions. It does not yet support an approved public valuation range. Key remaining limitations are subsidiary legal distribution capacity, intra-year liquidity, future refinancing costs, current economic share claims and changes since June. The ordinary-share count at October 31 and the interim diluted-average denominator are dated and not interchangeable.

Sources: AIA 2024 and 2025 annual reports, 2026 interim report, October 30 business update, current investor calendar and share-information page. Direct primary links accompany this report.
