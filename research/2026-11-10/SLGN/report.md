# SLGN: proposed Trivium partnership, not yet a completed transformation

Research update, 10 November 2026. Continues the November 7 dossier. No entry approval or cleared combined valuation.

The November 9 scanner records $42.13 and Opportunity 80. Silgan's proposed partnership would retain 60% ownership while combining contributed metal-container and flat-cap-closure businesses with Trivium. The retained dispensing and custom-container operations require a separate capital and cash-flow analysis. Closing is expected in mid-2027, subject to conditions; no exact next earnings date was verified on the official upcoming-events page.

The filed presentation reports June-LTM combined MCC adjusted EBITDA of $951.5m before a $225m year-three synergy target. Its illustrative $4.6bn net debt is about 4.83 times pre-synergy EBITDA. Those are dated adjusted measures, not verified post-close free cash flow.

The agreement matters beyond the 60/40 headline. AIP receives priority distributions diverted from Silgan's share and has conditional liquidity rights after four years. Their contractual pricing, potential cash requirements and limited share-settlement alternative need explicit modeling. A spin-off is a possible path, not a completed event or guaranteed escape from a timely put.

The remaining work is a consistent closing cash/debt bridge, separate parent and partnership reserves, seasonal liquidity, incentive claims and target replacement investment. Annual cash generation alone does not prove that money is available when required. Historical legacy valuation diagnostics should not be read as a current combined fair value.
