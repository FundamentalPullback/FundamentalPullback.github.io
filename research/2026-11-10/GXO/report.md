# GXO: growth, lease investment and the Wincanton perimeter

TTM revenue reached $13.836bn, while operating cash flow was $433m versus $450m in the comparable prior TTM. Investor Day is confirmed for November 16 at 09:00 Eastern. Cash capex does not capture all investment. In nine months, GXO reported $204m cash capex and $207m of finance-lease assets received in exchange for liabilities. Their combined $411m is an investment diagnostic, not a verified measure of homogeneous gross physical investment. The current quarter and half-year disclosures also identify a material $89m lease beginning April 1, already included in subsequent period totals.

The older lease bridge remains unresolved: FY2025 finance-lease additions of $74m are below the original nine-month figure of $83m. The resulting negative fourth-quarter arithmetic must not be treated as recurring negative investment. It does not by itself establish an accounting error. Forward investment estimates need to be explicit scenarios rather than a mechanical extension of this difference.

The CMA confirms the Wincanton grocery disposal closed September 13 to DP World Logistics UK. GXO paid $38m in cash; that is not sale proceeds. The cash payment already appears in the September balance and should not be deducted again. The annual net disposal charge of $34m differs from the note's specific $37m grocery write-down. The TTM net disposal charge is $58m. The $30m early lease-termination benefit also needs separation from recurring operating profit. Exact disposed-contract sales and profit and the portion of synergy already realized remain insufficiently disclosed for a precise continuing-perimeter forecast.

September financial debt of $2.818bn includes $487m finance leases. Operating lease liabilities of $3.002bn should not be deducted again if their ongoing costs remain in operating profit. Unrestricted cash was $340m, with restricted cash of $3m excluded. The company had $793m revolving capacity net of letters of credit, but the line expires in 2029. A $275m term loan matures in May 2027. Refinancing and seasonal operating cash requirements remain material assumptions, not automatic sources of shareholder value.

Newly checked November 2 legal shares total 113.029173m, versus the quarter's 114.636m diluted weighted-average shares. They are not interchangeable exact denominators. Stock compensation remains an economic cost. Pension surplus is not automatically available cash, and gross receivables sold under factoring programs are not an outstanding debt balance.

The principal next questions are total investment by contract, finance-lease modifications and payment schedules, continuing Wincanton earnings, realized savings, seasonal liquidity and sustainable returns on growth capital. Conditional capital tests remain highly sensitive to these assumptions. There is no cleared valuation range or entry approval in this update.
